- `ModalStack::Focusable` is removed (#2910): `ModalStack::push_with_focusable`/
  `ModalStack::push_float`'s internals take a plain `bool` again instead
  of the `Focusable::Yes`/`Focusable::No` enum; `ModalEntry::focusable`
  is unchanged (it was always `bool`). `ModalStack::push_focusable`
  (deprecated since 0.9.0, removal target "not before a 0.10.0 bump" per
  #2178) is also removed now that the crate is past 0.10.0 — use
  `push_with_focusable` directly.
