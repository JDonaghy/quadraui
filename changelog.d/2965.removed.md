- `Editor`, `Minimap` and `Image` paint only through the widget contract
  (#2965). Breaking: `Backend::draw_editor`, `set_editor_v_scrollbar_width`,
  `editor_v_scrollbar_width`, `set_editor_right_reserve_w`,
  `editor_right_reserve_w`, `editor_layout`, `editor_col_at_x`,
  `draw_minimap`, `minimap_layout`, `minimap_scale`, `set_minimap_scale`,
  `minimap_sizing`, `set_minimap_sizing` and `draw_image` are removed, along
  with `EditorPaintResult`, `MinimapPaintResult`, `Surface::Editor`,
  `Surface::Minimap`, `Surface::Image`, `FrameZone::Editor`,
  `FrameZone::Minimap`, `FrameZone::Image` and the per-backend
  editor/minimap/image rasteriser modules and their re-exports
  (`tui::draw_editor[_with_reserve]`, `tui::EditorPaintResult`,
  `tui::draw_minimap[_with_scale|_with_sizing]`,
  `tui::tui_minimap_layout[_with_sizing]`, `tui::COLS_PER_CELL`,
  `tui::LINES_PER_ROW`, `tui::draw_image`, `gtk::draw_editor`,
  `gtk::editor_col_at_x`, `gtk::draw_minimap`, `gtk::gtk_minimap_layout`,
  `gtk::draw_image`, `macos::editor`, `macos::minimap`, `macos::image`,
  `macos::draw_editor`, `macos::mac_minimap_layout`, `win::draw_editor`,
  `win::draw_minimap`, `win::win_minimap_layout`, `win::draw_image`). Paint
  with `DrawsPrimitives::draw_primitive`/`draw_dyn` (or push
  `Surface::Widget`) and read geometry with
  `DrawsPrimitives::layout_primitive`. The editor's scrollbar width and
  right reserve ride on `Editor::paint_options`, the minimap's scale and
  sizing on `Minimap::scale`/`Minimap::sizing`, and a bar or underline
  caret reaches the terminal through `CellSurface::set_cursor`. Click
  columns resolve monospace (`EditorLayout::col_at_x`). A captured editor,
  minimap or image reports `FrameZone::Widget`; a minimap is no longer a
  `ScreenLayout` tab stop (its widget contract takes no keyboard focus).
