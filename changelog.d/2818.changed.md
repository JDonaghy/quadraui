- Widget-contract type erasure (#2818): added `primitive::DynWidget` (a
  type-erased view of any `Interactive` widget, blanket-implemented for
  every `Interactive` implementor) and `DrawsPrimitives::draw_dyn`, and
  `frame::Surface` gained a `Widget { rect, widget: Box<dyn DynWidget> }`
  variant that paints through it. `CellGrid`'s dedicated `Surface::CellGrid`
  variant (and its `FrameZone::CellGrid` twin) are **removed**; CellGrid now
  paints through `Surface::Widget`, and its hit-test zone is
  `FrameZone::Widget { idx, id }` instead. `primitive::PrimitiveLayout` (the
  struct pairing a primitive's absolute paint area with its primitive-local
  layout) is renamed to `primitive::AreaLayout`, freeing that name for the
  new object-safe `PrimitiveLayout` trait `DynWidget::layout` returns.
  `primitive::PaintEnv` no longer carries a `target` field — `DynWidget::paint`
  takes `PaintTarget` as its own parameter instead. `Primitive` gained three
  defaulted methods (`id`, `font_role`, `measure`) and one defaulted
  `capture` method that `DynWidget`'s blanket impl delegates to.
