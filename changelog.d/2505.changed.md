- TUI's `TabBar` rasteriser (`tui::tab_bar`) is now a thin surface over a
  shared painter instead of a bespoke copy of tab-bar layout and paint
  (#2505). Its hit geometry comes from the shared
  `backend::tab_bar_hits_from_layout` + `backend::shift_tab_bar_hits`
  helpers — the same pair `TuiBackend::tab_bar_layout_icons`'s no-paint
  twin already uses — rather than hand-built
  `slot_positions`/`close_bounds`/`right_segment_bounds`, and the paint
  loop itself moved into `primitives::tab_bar::cell_paint::paint`, the
  cell-grid counterpart of the `native_surface_paint::paint` that GTK
  already paints through, so both of the primitive's painters live beside
  each other. `tui::draw_tab_bar_icons_with_chrome_and_glyphs` is that
  shared painter (re-exported under its existing name), the three narrower
  `tui::draw_tab_bar*` entry points still fill in TUI's defaults, and the
  cell-measure constants (`TAB_CLOSE_CHAR`, `TAB_CLOSE_COLS`,
  `TAB_SEPARATOR_CHAR`, `TAB_SEPARATOR_COLS`) now live with the primitive
  and are re-exported from `tui::` under the same names. `quadraui/src/tui`
  loses 392 non-test lines.
- The painted and no-paint TUI tab-bar paths now return identical,
  tab-indexed hit vectors (`slot_positions.len() == bar.tabs.len()`,
  indexed by `tab_idx`), which differs from the old hand-rolled vectors
  once tabs scroll or clip — a test pins that shape. Everything else is
  behaviour-preserving: every pre-existing `tui::tab_bar` test, including
  the close-glyph, bracket-framing, icon-reservation and reverse-video
  focus tests, passes unchanged.

  ## Downstream impact

  Additive only. `draw_tab_bar_icons_with_chrome_and_glyphs` keeps its
  name, signature and `tui::` path (now as a re-export of
  `primitives::tab_bar::cell_paint::paint`, which is `pub` +
  `#[doc(hidden)]` so the painter is callable but off the documented
  surface); the four constants keep their `tui::` paths and gain
  `primitives::tab_bar::` ones. No consumer hit:
  `grep -rn 'draw_tab_bar\|TAB_CLOSE_COLS\|TAB_CLOSE_CHAR\|TAB_SEPARATOR'
  ~/src/vimcode/src ~/src/coord-tui/src` finds only comments plus
  vimcode's own `render::tab_bar::TAB_CLOSE_COLS`, and both consumers paint
  through `Backend::draw_tab_bar_icons` rather than these free functions.
  `grep -rn 'slot_positions\|TabBarHits' ~/src/vimcode/src` shows vimcode
  has migrated off `TabBarHits` entirely (only doc-comment mentions
  remain), and the sealed oracle's hit loop
  (`tests/acceptance/ms-11/wide_tab_labels.rs:129`) already filters
  sentinels with `end > start`, so it is index-safe either way.
