- `Interactive::focus_ring` and `FocusIndicator` (#3108): a widget names where
  the host's focus ring goes (its bounds, a rect inside them, or nowhere
  because it shows its own focus). `PaintedWidget::focus_ring` and
  `primitive::painted_focus_ring` read it back for the frame.
  `frame::draw_widget` paints one widget the way `Surface::Widget` does,
  focus included.
