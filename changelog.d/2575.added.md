- `TuiBackend::register_opaque_rect` lets a rasteriser mark a cell rect as
  painted with non-text content this frame — the seam a terminal-graphics
  rasteriser's placeholder cells wire into (#2575). Post-paint passes skip
  those cells instead of corrupting them: `apply_selection_highlight`
  leaves their fg/bg alone (swapping it would scramble a placeholder cell
  whose foreground colour encodes an image id, not a real colour) and
  substitutes the registered `fallback_text` once per contiguous run
  instead of copying the raw placeholder codepoints; `TuiDriver::inventory`
  reports the rect as a zone rather than a text run, and `screen`/
  `screen_contains` never surface the placeholder. A later paint over part
  of a registered rect (a menu, toast, or dialog opened on top in the same
  frame) wins those cells back for ordinary text behaviour automatically,
  with no need to re-register or shrink the rect.
