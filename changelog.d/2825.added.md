- `ProgressBar`, `Spinner` and `Scrollbar` implement the widget contract
  (#2825): `Primitive` (`measure`, `layout`, the shared pixel paint and a
  new cell painter written against `CellSurface`, both living in the
  widget's own module) plus `Interactive` — `non_interactive!` for the
  two indicators, and a real impl for `Scrollbar`. Each can now be pushed
  as a `Surface::Widget`, registered in a `FrameTree`, and decoded from
  the wire by `decode_widget` under its existing `"progress"` /
  `"spinner"` / `"scrollbar"` kind name. `quadraui::tui::{draw_progress,
  draw_spinner, draw_scrollbar}` are now thin delegates onto the
  widget-owned cell painters.
- `Scrollbar` answers its own gestures: a thumb drag (through
  `EventCx::capture_pointer`, so it keeps tracking after the pointer
  leaves the one-column gutter), a bare-track click that pages by one
  viewport, and a wheel notch worth
  `SCROLLBAR_WHEEL_VIEWPORTS_PER_NOTCH` of a viewport. All three resolve
  to the new `UiEvent::ScrollbarMoved { widget, fraction }` — a fraction
  of the thumb's travel, not an item index, because the descriptor is
  geometry-only and the host owns the content extent. New public
  `ScrollbarLayout`, `ScrollbarState` and `ScrollbarEvent` types carry
  the resolved geometry, the hover/drag state a renderer derives, and the
  action.
