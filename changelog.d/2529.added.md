- GTK backend now emits `UiEvent::SystemThemeChanged` when the OS light/dark
  preference flips live (#2529): connects `gtk4::Settings`' own
  `notify::gtk-application-prefer-dark-theme` signal, plus a best-effort
  watch on the `org.freedesktop.portal.Settings` `SettingChanged` signal for
  desktops that only speak the portal's colour-scheme preference with no
  GTK-side settings bridge. The two producers share one dedup check so a
  flip observed by both still dispatches exactly once.
