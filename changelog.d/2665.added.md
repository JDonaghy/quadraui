- `CanvasEvent` (`RegionClicked`/`RegionDoubleClicked`/`RegionHovered`) and
  `UiEvent::Canvas(WidgetId, CanvasEvent)` (#2665) surface a `Canvas`'s
  declared `regions` (#2664) as typed click/hover events. `quadraui-binding-core`
  resolves a region hit through a new `HitDetail::CanvasRegion`, makes
  `Canvas::focusable` drive its `ExtraPaint::tab_stop`, and wires
  `CanvasEvent`/the `CanvasRegion` `HitDetail` kind into the Python/Node
  wire schema (`wire.pyi`/`wire.d.ts` regenerated via
  `scripts/gen_wire_types.py`).
