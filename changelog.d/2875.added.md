- `Form` is on the widget contract (#2875): it implements `Primitive`
  (one `measure`, one unit-branching `layout`, one pixel paint and one
  cell paint) and `Interactive` (`FocusSpec::Children`, one stop per
  field; Tab/Shift-Tab traversal with scroll-into-view; per-`FieldKind`
  keys answered by that field's own widget; click routing and the slider
  drag), so it can reach every backend through
  `DrawsPrimitives::draw_primitive`/`draw_dyn` and sit in a `FrameTree`
  as `Surface::Widget`. New public items: `FormState` with its
  `TextCursor` / `DateCursor` / `ItemCursor` parts, and
  `FormPrimitiveLayout`. The cell-grid layout resolver and rasteriser
  move from `tui::form` to `primitives::form::{cell_layout, cell_paint}`,
  which `tui::form` now re-exports unchanged; the keyboard, focus and
  click behaviour moves out of `compose::FormController`, which keeps its
  public surface and drives the same implementation.
