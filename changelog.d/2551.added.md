- `DataTable::selection_mode` and `DataTable::selected_indices` (#2551):
  in `SelectionMode::Multi`, Ctrl/Cmd and Shift clicks, Shift+Up/Down,
  Ctrl/Cmd+A and Ctrl+Space/Insert grow a multi-selection reported as
  `DataTableEvent::SelectionSet`, and `selected_indices` paints it back
  (`DataTable::is_row_selected`). `Single` (the default) treats a modified
  click as a plain one. `SelectionCore` gains `press`, `key`, `extend_by`
  and `sync_painted` (it follows a set the app changes through the
  descriptor) and a `painted` field. A `ListView` takes Ctrl+Space and
  Insert as toggles alongside its existing multi-select gestures; plain
  Space still activates.
