- `Tooltip`, `Dialog` and `ToastOverlay` report their drop-shadow extent
  plus border width as `Primitive::paint_bleed` (#3054), so
  `draw_dyn`/`draw_primitive` no longer clip the shadow to the box on GTK,
  macOS and Win. `Tooltip` bleeds only when its chrome border is `Full`
  (the only card that paints a shadow); `Dialog` uses its modal shadow
  elevation floor. `RichTextPopup`, `Completions` and `Float` paint no
  shadow and are unchanged.
