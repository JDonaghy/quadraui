- `Style::list_cell_overflow` / `Style::with_list_cell_overflow` (#3066):
  how a cell-grid `ListView` painted through the widget contract cuts a row
  too long for the list — `CellOverflow::Ellipsis` (default) ends it in
  `…`, `CellOverflow::Clip` cuts it at the last cell with no marker, as
  0.1.2 did. Also a `"style"` override key on the Python/Node bindings
  (`"list_cell_overflow": "ellipsis" | "clip"`).
