- The tree-family legacy paint path is removed (#2957): the `Backend` methods
  `draw_tree`, `draw_tree_with_state`, `tree_layout`, `tree_vscrollbar`,
  `tree_paints_own_scrollbar`, `tree_scroll_row_height`, `draw_tree_table`,
  `tree_table_layout`, `draw_settings_chrome` and `draw_solid_fill`; the
  per-backend `tui`/`gtk`/`macos`/`win` `tree.rs` rasteriser files and
  `tui/solid_fill.rs` (with their `tui::draw_tree`, `tui::draw_tree_table`,
  `tui::draw_solid_fill`, `gtk::draw_tree`, `macos::draw_tree`,
  `win::draw_tree` and `*_tree_layout` re-exports); `Surface::Tree`,
  `FrameZone::Tree` and `ScreenLayout::tree_frame` (a captured
  `OwnedContent::Tree` now maps to `FrameZone::Widget`); `compose::
  TreeController`, `TreeControllerEvent`, `TreeNavKey`,
  `classify_tree_nav_key` and `TreeHoverTracker`. Paint a `TreeView` or
  `TreeTable` with `DrawsPrimitives::draw_dyn`/`draw_primitive`, read its
  geometry with `layout_primitive`, drive it through its `Interactive`
  (or `behaviour::nav::TreeNavCore` for bare path arithmetic), and paint a
  plain colour block with a `Canvas` `DrawOp::Rect`. The settings chrome
  paints through `compose::sidebar_panel_body::paint_settings_chrome`/
  `paint_settings_chrome_cells`. `layout_metrics::tree_layout`/
  `tree_layout_measured` are renamed `pixel_tree_layout`/
  `pixel_tree_layout_measured`.
- `compose::ChatController`, `ChatControllerAction` and the deprecated
  `ChatControllerEvent` are removed, and `compose::chat_controller` is
  renamed `compose::chat` (#2957). Own a `Chat` descriptor instead: paint
  it with `Surface::Widget`/`draw_dyn`, register it in
  `AppLogic::frame_tree` (the runtime keeps its `ChatState`), and apply
  every `UiEvent::Chat` with `Chat::apply`. `Chat::clear_input`,
  `Chat::toggle_turn_collapsed` and `Chat::set_turn_summary` replace the
  controller's helpers.
- `compose::DualModePaletteController` and the deprecated
  `DualModePaletteEvent` are removed (#2957). Own a `DualModePalette`,
  route it through `AppLogic::frame_tree`, and store each
  `UiEvent::DualModePalette` with `DualModePalette::apply`
  (`set_items`, `with_mode` and `with_vim_keys` cover the controller's
  setters).
- `MessageDialogController`, `MessageDialogEvent`, `FormDialogController`
  and `FormDialogEvent` are removed (#2957). Own a `MessageDialog` or
  `FormDialog` and its `*State`: on the runtime's routing engine push it
  as a `Surface::Widget` over the viewport and register it in
  `AppLogic::frame_tree`; a host that reads raw `UiEvent`s paints it with
  `draw_dyn` over `backend.viewport().rect()` (`FormDialog::painted(&state)`
  for the form dialog) and resolves each event with `handle_event(&mut
  state, &event, backend)`. The binding-core interaction engine's
  `open_form_dialog` takes a `FormDialog`, and `form_dialog_mut` returns
  one.
