- The tree-family legacy paint path is removed (#2957): the `Backend` methods
  `draw_tree`, `draw_tree_with_state`, `tree_layout`, `tree_vscrollbar`,
  `tree_paints_own_scrollbar`, `tree_scroll_row_height`, `draw_tree_table`,
  `tree_table_layout`, `draw_settings_chrome` and `draw_solid_fill`; the
  per-backend `tui`/`gtk`/`macos`/`win` `tree.rs` rasteriser files and
  `tui/solid_fill.rs` (with their `tui::draw_tree`, `tui::draw_tree_table`,
  `tui::draw_solid_fill`, `gtk::draw_tree`, `macos::draw_tree`,
  `win::draw_tree` and `*_tree_layout` re-exports); `Surface::Tree`,
  `FrameZone::Tree` and `ScreenLayout::tree_frame` (a captured
  `OwnedContent::Tree` now maps to `FrameZone::Widget`); `compose::
  TreeController`, `TreeControllerEvent`, `TreeNavKey`,
  `classify_tree_nav_key` and `TreeHoverTracker`. Paint a `TreeView` or
  `TreeTable` with `DrawsPrimitives::draw_dyn`/`draw_primitive`, read its
  geometry with `layout_primitive`, drive it through its `Interactive`
  (or `behaviour::nav::TreeNavCore` for bare path arithmetic), and paint a
  plain colour block with a `Canvas` `DrawOp::Rect`. The settings chrome
  paints through `compose::sidebar_panel_body::paint_settings_chrome`/
  `paint_settings_chrome_cells`. `layout_metrics::tree_layout`/
  `tree_layout_measured` are renamed `pixel_tree_layout`/
  `pixel_tree_layout_measured`.
