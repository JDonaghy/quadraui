- The crate-private `compose::divider_drag` module and the
  `primitives::tab_bar::draw_tab_strip` helper, both without callers now
  that `TabGroup` drags and paints through its `SplitTree` and `TabBar`
  children (#2955).
- **Breaking:** `TabGroupController`, its `TabGroupLayout` and
  `PaneDragRect`, and the `Pane` / `PaneTab` aliases are removed (#2957).
  Host a `TabGroup` directly: paint it as a widget, register it in the
  app's `FrameTree`, and write each `UiEvent::TabGroup` intent back with
  `TabGroup::apply`; the layout's `content_bounds` are where each pane's
  body paints. Pane keyboard focus is the runtime's (one stop per pane),
  and a host that needs its own notion of a "current pane" tracks
  `PaneFocused` itself.
