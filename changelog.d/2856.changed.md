- `CellGrid`'s `Primitive::layout` impl is re-ported onto `LayoutCtx::units()`
  (#2856): its TUI-vs-pixel-backend discriminator now checks
  `ctx.units() == Units::Cells` instead of the bare `line_height <= 1.0`
  float comparison. Unlike `ListView`/`TreeView`/`DataTable`, `Primitive::paint`
  has no `crate::primitive::PaintState` to thread through — `CellGrid`'s
  selection, cursor and focus are already persistent fields on the primitive
  itself rather than transient backend-tracked hover/press state, so there is
  nothing for a `PaintState` impl to add. Pure refactor — painted output is
  unchanged.
