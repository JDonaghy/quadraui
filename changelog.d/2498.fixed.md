- `Form`'s `FieldKind::Dropdown` ("Select") and `FieldKind::ComboBox` trailing
  chevrons now paint the codicon `chevron-down` glyph on GTK/macOS/Win
  instead of the plain Unicode `▾` text glyph (#2498), matching
  `TreeView`/section-header chevrons' own codicon substitution; TUI's own
  cell-grid `tui::form` renderer still paints the plain `▾`.
