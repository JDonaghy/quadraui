- `FormController` now gives a `Form`-embedded `FieldKind::Slider` field a
  real click/drag/key arm (#2587): on a field with no label column to its
  left (an empty label, or a resolved `LabelPosition::Hidden`) a click or
  drag on the field's row maps the pointer's x position to a value;
  Left/Down, Right/Up, Home/End step or jump a focused `Slider` field's
  value regardless of its label, each emitting `FormEvent::SliderChanged`
  — previously `form_click_event` fell through to
  `FormEvent::FocusChanged` and there was no key handling at all, so the
  only way to move a `Slider` field's value was the a11y
  Increment/Decrement action. A labeled `Slider` field's click still only
  focuses it until a backend grows a real `item_bounds` entry for its
  track.
