- `FormController` now gives a `Form`-embedded `FieldKind::Slider` field a
  real click/drag/key arm (#2587): a click or drag on the field's row maps
  the pointer's x position to a value, and Left/Down, Right/Up, Home/End
  step or jump it, each emitting `FormEvent::SliderChanged` — previously
  `form_click_event` fell through to `FormEvent::FocusChanged` and there
  was no key handling at all, so the only way to move a `Slider` field's
  value was the a11y Increment/Decrement action.
