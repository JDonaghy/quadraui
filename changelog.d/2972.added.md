- `ScrollView` implements `A11yPrimitive`, `Primitive` and `Interactive`
  (#2972): it lays out a content clip beside a scrollbar gutter, paints the
  scrollbar through `Scrollbar`'s own painter, and turns the wheel,
  PageUp/PageDown/Home/End and arrow keys, and thumb drags into the new
  `UiEvent::ScrollView(id, ScrollViewEvent::Scrolled { offset })`. The
  offset stays the descriptor's. New public items: `ScrollViewLayout`,
  `ScrollViewState`, `primitives::scroll_view::{paint_clipped,
  draw_scrollbar_for}`. The `"scroll_view"` binding kind decodes as a
  generic widget leaf.
