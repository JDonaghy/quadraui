- `Split` and `SplitTree` implement `Primitive` and `Interactive` (#2968), so
  both reach a frame as `Surface::Widget` and paint through `draw_dyn`: one
  shared pixel sash painter, and one cell painter with junction glyphs that
  a `Split` shares as the two-leaf tree it is. A left press on a divider
  captures the pointer and sets the resize cursor, and every captured move
  becomes `UiEvent::SplitDividerDragged`. New public items:
  `SplitDividerDrag`, `SplitState`, `SplitTreeState`, `Split::cell_layout`,
  `SplitTree::cell_layout`, `SplitLayout::ratio_at`, `SplitLayout::divider`,
  `SplitTreeDivider::ratio_at` and `SplitTreeDivider::band`.
