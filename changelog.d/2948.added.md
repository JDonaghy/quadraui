- `TreeTable` implements the widget contract (#2948): `Primitive` lays
  out and paints in the surface's own units (cells and pixels), composed
  over `DataTable`'s own layout and painters, and `Interactive` is
  controlled. Keys go through `TreeNavCore` (Up/Down/Page/Home/End, `j`/`k`
  when the new `TreeTable::vim_keys` is set, Left/Right fold and walk the
  tree, Enter activates, Space toggles); chevron presses, the scrollbar
  (thumb drag and track paging) and the wheel are handled by the tree
  table; header clicks, divider drags, row selection, context menus,
  type-to-jump and select-all go through `DataTable`'s own `Interactive`
  impl and come back as `TreeTableEvent`s with each row's path. New
  `TreeTableState`, `TreeTablePrimitiveLayout` and `TreeTableSubPart`.
- Lazy children are descriptor content: `TreeTableNode::children_pending`
  marks a branch whose children are not supplied yet (expanding it reports
  the new `TreeTableEvent::ChildrenRequested` once, until the flag
  clears), and `TreeTableNode::placeholder` marks the loading row that
  stands in for them (never selected or activated). New
  `TreeTableEvent::{ScrollChanged, ChildrenRequested,
  ContextMenuRequested}`.
- `TreeNavCore::press_scrollbar` (with `ScrollbarPress`), the scrollbar
  press `TreeView`, `TreeController` and `TreeTable` share, and
  `TreeNavCore::handle_key` (with `TreeKey`), the tree key dispatch
  `TreeView` and `TreeTable` share; its vim gate ignores `j`/`k` while
  Ctrl, Alt or Cmd is held.
