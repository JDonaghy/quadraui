- `Chat` is a composite widget on the widget contract (#2949). The
  transcript, status strip and hint paint through `MessageList`, the busy
  indicator through `Spinner`, the transcript scrollbar through
  `Scrollbar`, the input through `TextInput` and the Send/Stop segment
  through `Button`, each driven through its own `Interactive`. `Chat` is
  controlled: the input buffer and caret, the transcript scroll position,
  the focused turn and the collapsed turns are descriptor fields, and
  gestures report `ChatEvent::InputChanged`, `TranscriptScrolled`,
  `FocusedTurnChanged` and `TurnCollapsed` alongside `Submit`,
  `Cancelled`, `StopRequested`, `KeyPressed` and `TurnClicked`
  (`Chat::apply` stores them). New `UiEvent::Chat(WidgetId, ChatEvent)`,
  `ChatState`, `ChatLayout` and `ChatZones`. `ChatController` keeps its
  API and drives a `Chat` underneath.
