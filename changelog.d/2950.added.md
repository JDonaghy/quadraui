- `MessageDialog`, `FormDialog` and `HelpOverlay` are widget-contract
  widgets (#2950), each with its `*State`, composing `Dialog`'s (and, for
  `FormDialog`, `Form`'s) own `Primitive` and `Interactive`. A form
  dialog's focus is one order across the form's field stops and its
  buttons. `HelpOverlayAction` reaches a runtime host as the new
  `UiEvent::HelpOverlay`; a `FormDialog`'s field events ride `UiEvent::Form`
  under the dialog's id.
