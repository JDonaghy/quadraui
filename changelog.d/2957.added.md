- `TreeView` paints a pixel-precise `scroll_px` from its paint state
  (`TreePaintState::with_scroll_px` handed to `draw_dyn`) on every backend,
  and `widget_kinds` registers `"tree"`, `"tree_table"` and
  `"tree_table_controller"` (#2957). `layout_metrics::
  pixel_tree_layout_with_scroll_px` lays a tree out against that position,
  measuring the chevron through the backend.
- `compose::drive_ui_event` (re-exported at the crate root with its
  `Driven` answer) drives any `Interactive` widget with one raw `UiEvent`,
  laid out exactly as `draw_dyn` paints it, for a host whose events do not
  pass through `AppLogic::frame_tree` — a `ShellApp` or a gallery demo
  (#2957). `DualModePalette::sync_caret` hands such a host's live query
  caret to the paint.
- `Viewport::rect` is the viewport as a rect at the origin. `MessageDialog`
  gains `handle_event`, `bounds`, `dialog_layout` and `focused_button_id`;
  `FormDialog` gains `handle_event`, `painted`, `bounds`, `dialog_layout`,
  `focused_button_id` and the `with_buttons` / `with_severity` /
  `with_vertical_buttons` / `with_body` builders (#2957).
