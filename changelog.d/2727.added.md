- Added `compose::CellGridController` (#2727): spreadsheet-style cursor
  movement and rectangular range selection for a `CellGrid` — arrows,
  Tab/Shift+Tab, Enter/Shift+Enter, Page Up/Down, Home/End, Ctrl+Home/End,
  Ctrl+Arrow, Shift-extend, click and click-drag range selection,
  row/column header and corner/Ctrl+A "select all" clicks, and
  keep-visible scrolling that respects frozen panes. Emits
  `CellGridControllerEvent::CursorMoved`/`SelectionChanged`; does no
  editing.
