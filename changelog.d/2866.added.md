- `Tooltip` and `Float` implement the widget contract (#2866):
  `Primitive` (layout, pixel paint and cell paint in the widget's own
  module, plus `Tooltip`'s natural-size `measure`) and `Interactive` via
  `non_interactive!`, so either can be pushed as a `Surface::Widget` and
  registered in a `FrameTree`. `Float` also implements `A11yPrimitive` (a
  "Floating panel" group). `quadraui::tui::draw_tooltip*`/`draw_float`
  are now thin delegates onto the widget-owned cell painters, which paint
  through `CellSurface`: tooltip text now advances by display width, so a
  double-width glyph takes two columns instead of overlapping its
  neighbour.
