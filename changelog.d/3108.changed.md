- The TUI focus ring no longer covers a focused control's content (#3108).
  It draws only in blank cells, stepping around a row of text, and a
  one-row control is underlined instead. A `Button` rings its `[ Label ]` at
  its natural width rather than its whole layout cell (`ButtonLayout`
  gains `label_rect`), and a focused button's brackets take the accent
  colour. A `Form` shows its own focused-row highlight with no host ring.
  `TextDisplay` is no longer a Tab stop unless it shows a scrollbar
  (`show_scrollbar`). A Rust app routing widgets through
  `AppLogic::frame_tree` alone now shows the ring on the widget a click
  focused, and `ScreenLayout::draw` tells each widget whether it holds
  focus (`PaintState::focus_ring_visible`), the same as the Python and Node
  hosts, so focus renders identically in all three languages.
