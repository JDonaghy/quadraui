- `TabBar` carries its active-tab chrome, per-tab icons and close/dirty glyph
  overrides as descriptor fields — `chrome: TabChrome`, `icons:
  Vec<Option<TabIcon>>`, `glyphs: TabBarGlyphs` (#2992) — **breaking**: three
  new fields on a struct consumers build with exhaustive literals (end the
  literal in `..Default::default()`). `TabBar`'s `Primitive` impl honours all
  three, so a chrome, icon or glyph tab strip paints through
  `DrawsPrimitives::draw_primitive`/`draw_dyn` with no
  `Backend::draw_tab_bar_with_chrome`/`draw_tab_bar_icons`/
  `draw_tab_bar_with_chrome_and_glyphs` call; `Primitive::layout` reserves the
  icon columns and the bracket frame those rasterisers reserve, and those
  `Backend` methods keep their signatures and read only their arguments. All
  three fields are optional on the wire (`#[serde(default)]`), and
  `TabBarGlyphs` gains `Serialize`/`Deserialize`, so `TabBar`'s py/node
  descriptor shape is unchanged.
