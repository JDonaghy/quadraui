- **Breaking:** the group-B widgets' bespoke `Backend` methods and
  `frame::Surface` variants are gone (#2876); every one of them now paints
  through the widget contract (`DrawsPrimitives::draw_primitive` /
  `draw_dyn`, or `Surface::Widget` from a `ScreenLayout`). Removed from
  `Backend`: `draw_text_display`/`text_display_layout`,
  `draw_text_input`/`text_input_layout`,
  `draw_text_field`/`text_field_layout`,
  `draw_command_line`/`draw_command_line_selection`/`command_line_layout`,
  `draw_completions`, `draw_dialog`, `draw_float`, `draw_tooltip`/
  `draw_tooltip_with_chrome`, `draw_rich_text_popup`/
  `draw_rich_text_popup_with_font_role`,
  `draw_toast_overlay`/`toast_stack_layout`, `draw_number_input`,
  `draw_combo_box` and `draw_date_picker`; removed from `frame::Surface`:
  `TextDisplay`, `TextInput`, `TextField`, `CommandLine`, `Completions`,
  `Dialog`, `Float`, `Tooltip`, `RichTextPopup` and `Toast`. The per-backend
  TUI wrapper modules behind them are deleted too. A host that called one of
  these calls `backend.draw_dyn(rect, &widget, &state)` (or
  `draw_primitive`) instead, and reads the resolved layout back off the
  return value; `Tooltip::chrome` and `RichTextPopup::font_role` carry what
  the `_with_chrome`/`_with_font_role` variants took as arguments. A host
  that relied on a deleted method registering a frame zone registers it
  itself with `Backend::register_zone`, as `TabGroupController::render`
  does.
- The macOS toast entrance slide is gone with
  `MacBackend::draw_toast_overlay` (#2876): a toast now paints at the
  resting position its own `ToastStackLayout` reports on the first frame it
  is shown, matching GTK, Windows and TUI. The animation hook the slide used
  (`primitives::toast::native_surface_paint::paint_with_x_offset`) is still
  there for a backend that wants to re-introduce it behind the contract.
