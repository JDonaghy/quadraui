- `Backend::draw_switch`/`Backend::switch_layout` and the per-backend
  `tui`/`macos`/`win` `switch` rasteriser modules are removed (#3006): `Switch`
  already paints and lays out through the widget contract (`Primitive` +
  `Interactive`, #3005), so every caller — `tui/form.rs`'s inline body,
  `examples/common/controls.rs`, the three `*_controls.rs` example runners and
  the four `*_standalone_controls_driver.rs`/`conformance/c0.rs` test suites —
  now calls `DrawsPrimitives::draw_primitive`/`layout_primitive` instead of a
  `Backend` method. `quadraui-binding-core` drops `Switch`'s `ExtraPaint` impl
  and its hand-written click/key dispatch arms in favor of the same generic
  `WIDGET_KINDS`/`PaintedHit::Widget` seam `IconGrid`/`CellGrid` already use;
  a `Switch` click or Enter/Space key now emits `UiEvent::SwitchToggled`
  instead of `UiEvent::Form(.., FormEvent::ToggleChanged)`. Neither removed
  `Backend` method had a caller in any published consumer.
