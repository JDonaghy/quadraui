- `Completions` and `RichTextPopup` implement the widget contract
  (#2867): `Primitive` (natural-size `measure`, layout, pixel paint and
  cell paint all in the widget's own module) plus `Interactive`, so
  either can be pushed as a `Surface::Widget` and registered in a
  `FrameTree`. `Completions` navigates with the arrow/Page/Home/End keys
  through `behaviour::nav::FlatNav`, scrolls the highlighted candidate
  into view with `behaviour::scroll::ensure_visible`, selects on click
  and accepts on double-click, Enter or Tab, and reports the new
  `CompletionsEvent`. `RichTextPopup` scrolls its body on the wheel and
  the Page keys, opens a clicked `RichTextLink`, and reports the new
  `RichTextPopupEvent`. `quadraui::tui::draw_completions`/
  `draw_rich_text_popup` are now thin delegates onto the widget-owned
  cell painters, which paint through `CellSurface`: a completion row's
  text advances by display width, so a double-width glyph takes two
  columns instead of overlapping its neighbour.
- Every pixel backend now paints `Completions` through the shared
  `primitives::completions` painter (#2867), so GTK and macOS gain the
  kind icon, every label span and the right-aligned `detail` they
  previously dropped, and all three backends stay in step with the TUI
  rendering.
