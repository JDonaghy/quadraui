- `Toolbar` is on the widget contract (#2872): it implements `Primitive`
  (one `layout` resolving the same `ToolbarLayout` in cells or pixels, a
  `paint` through the shared pixel painter, a `paint_cells` through the
  cell rasteriser that moved out of `src/tui/toolbar.rs` into
  `primitives/toolbar/{cell_layout,cell_paint}.rs`) and `Interactive`
  (hover/press tracking in a new `ToolbarState`, which is also a
  `PaintState`; click-to-activate through the descriptor's own
  `Toolbar::click`; Left/Right/Up/Down/Home/End over the enabled items
  via `behaviour::nav::FlatNav`, skipping separators, inline labels,
  spacers and disabled actions). A bar can now be pushed as
  `Surface::Widget` and registered in a `FrameTree` instead of reaching a
  backend through `Backend::draw_toolbar_interactive`. The new
  `ToolbarSubPart` names a button slot, a `Group` child, a menu button's
  dropdown arrow and the overflow chevron for `painted_sub_center` and
  driver targeting, and `ToolbarEvent` gains `MenuRequested` and
  `OverflowRequested` for the two activations it had no vocabulary for.
