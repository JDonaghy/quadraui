- `UiEvent::DoubleClick` carries the `button` that was double-clicked, and
  `CanvasEvent::RegionDoubleClicked` carries it too (#3074). The shared
  `DoubleClickDetector` folds only left presses, so two quick right-clicks
  on a row are two `ContextMenuRequested`s rather than a double-click on
  every backend. A double-click a widget ignores reaches it again as a
  plain press (`DynWidget::handle`, via the new
  `RoutedEvent::double_click_as_press`), so two quick clicks on a `Button`
  activate it twice, live and in every driver.
