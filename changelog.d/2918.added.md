- `CellGrid` now implements `Interactive` for real (#2918): keyboard
  navigation (arrows, Tab/Shift+Tab, Enter/Shift+Enter, Page Up/Down,
  Home/End and their Ctrl-jump variants, Ctrl+A, F2, plain typing) and
  mouse click/drag over cells, column/row headers and the corner, routed
  through the generic `Interactive`/`RoutedEvent` runtime instead of
  `non_interactive!`. `CellGridEvent` gains `Navigated`,
  `CellEditRequested`, `CellEditStarted`, `CellEditChanged`,
  `CellEditCommitted` and `CellEditCancelled` variants carrying the
  cursor/selection/scroll/edit state a host applies back onto its own
  `CellGrid`, and a new `CellGridState` (`Interactive::State`) tracks the
  in-progress mouse drag anchor. `crate::compose::CellGridController` is
  unchanged and keeps working exactly as before — the two coexist as
  alternative ways to drive the same primitive.
