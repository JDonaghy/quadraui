- `Backend` holds platform services only (#2981): exactly 66 methods, none
  of them a widget's `draw_*` or `*_layout`. `Backend::scrollbar_reserve` is
  gone (it returned 8.0 on GTK and 0 elsewhere). A host that reserved
  toolkit overlay-scrollbar room for its own content keeps its own
  constant; a host whose own scrollbar gutter is already wider than 8 px
  drops the `.max(backend.scrollbar_reserve())` term with no pixel change.
- `frame::Surface` has exactly two variants (#2981): `Widget`, and the new
  `Opaque { rect }`, a rect with a z-order slot and an empty hit zone but no
  paint.
- `FrameZone::TabBar` is gone (#2981); a tab bar's zone is
  `FrameZone::Widget`.
- `OwnedContent::List` and `OwnedContent::FindReplace` are gone (#2981). A
  captured `ListView` is `OwnedContent::ListWidget`; a captured
  `FindReplacePanel` is `OwnedContent::Opaque`, which the accessibility tree
  still names from the panel's own `a11y_info` ("Find and replace") and
  which hit-tests as `FrameZone::Empty` in a replayed frame.
- `src/{tui,gtk,macos,win}/` hold platform modules only (#2981). The TUI
  braille dot packing moves from `tui::braille` to
  `cell_surface::braille`.
