- `quadraui-binding-core`'s generic `DynWidget` leaf seam (#2904): a layout-tree
  leaf can now carry `LeafDescriptor::Widget { kind, widget: Rc<dyn DynWidget> }`,
  decoded through a new `crate::extra_paint::WIDGET_KINDS` table (parallel to
  `EXTRA_PRIMITIVE_KINDS`, but routed through `quadraui::decode_widget` instead
  of a bespoke `ExtraPaint` impl), painted via `DrawsPrimitives::draw_dyn`,
  hit-tested as a whole area (`HitDetail::WidgetBody`), and dispatched to
  `DynWidget::handle` by `InteractionEngine`, backed by a new per-widget
  `quadraui::WidgetStateStore`. `"icon_grid"` is the seam's first member:
  `IconGrid`'s old `ExtraPaint` impl and its `EXTRA_PRIMITIVE_KINDS` entry are
  removed, so it now decodes and paints through this generic path instead of a
  kind-specific one, both as a `LayoutLeafNode` leaf and as a flat top-level
  `RenderFrameDict` slot key — the same two shapes its old `ExtraPaint` impl
  supported. `IconGrid` also gains a real `Interactive::focus` returning
  `FocusSpec::Stop`, so it stays a keyboard tab stop and click-to-focus target
  through the generic path exactly as it was through its old
  `ExtraPaint::tab_stop` override.
