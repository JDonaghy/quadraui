- `Minimap` gains `scale: MinimapScale` and `sizing: Option<MinimapSizing>`
  (#2963), both `#[serde(default)]`; `Minimap` is not `#[non_exhaustive]`,
  so a struct literal must name them. `MinimapScale` and `MinimapSizing`
  derive serde, and the wire `MinimapDescriptor` gains optional `scale` and
  `sizing` keys.
- The minimap glyph atlas is cached per thread in `primitives::minimap`,
  keyed on the sampled glyph sheet and the tile size, instead of in a field
  on `GtkBackend` and `MacBackend` (#2963).
- Win paints a `MinimapScale::Two` minimap as column blocks rather than
  DirectWrite text runs (#2963): its surface rasterises no glyph sheet.
