- `BoardModel` and `PipelineView` implement `Primitive` and `Interactive`
  (#2969) and register as the `board` and `pipeline_view` widget kinds in
  the bindings. A board reports `UiEvent::Board(id, BoardAction)` for its
  navigation keys, a card click (`SelectCard`), double-click (`OpenIssue`)
  and right-click (`ContextMenu`). A pipeline reports
  `UiEvent::PipelineView(id, PipelineEvent)`: Left/Right report the new
  `PipelineEvent::StageFocused`, Enter and clicks select a stage or fire its
  action. `focused_stage`, `selected_card_id` and the scroll offsets stay
  descriptor fields the app writes back. New public items:
  `BoardModel::cell_layout`, `PipelineView::cell_layout` and
  `PipelineViewPrimitiveLayout`.
