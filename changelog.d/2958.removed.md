- The `AppShell` and `BottomPanelController` owners are removed (#2958),
  along with `BottomPanelLayout`. Use the `ShellView` and `BottomPanel`
  descriptors directly. `ShellView` now carries every `AppShell` builder,
  accessor and mutator (`with_status_bar`, `show_panel`,
  `toggle_sidebar`, `add_panel`, `activity_select_next`, ...). Its
  gesture state is a `ShellViewState` the host keeps beside it:
  `AppShell::render(backend, area)` becomes
  `ShellView::render(&state, backend, area)`,
  `AppShell::handle(event, backend, area)` becomes
  `ShellView::handle_event(&mut state, event, backend, area)`,
  `AppShell::layout(area, line_height)` becomes
  `ShellView::shell_layout(area, line_height)`, and
  `AppShell::bottom_band_bounds()` becomes
  `ShellView::bottom_band_bounds(area, line_height, style)` /
  `bottom_band_bounds_for(backend, area)`. A plain
  `draw_dyn(area, &shell, &())` also paints the shell and publishes its
  `app-shell:*` chrome zones. `AppShell::hovered_activity_idx` is
  `ShellViewState::activity.hovered_idx`, and `ShellContext` exposes it
  as `hovered_activity_idx()`. A bottom panel is now a `BottomPanel`
  (`BottomPanel::from_config`) with a `BottomPanelState`:
  `BottomPanel::click(&mut state, &layout, position)` resolves a click
  against the `BottomPanelPrimitiveLayout` that `draw_dyn` returns, and
  `BottomPanel::apply` applies what it reports. `close_tab`,
  `activate_tab` and `toggle_maximised` move onto `BottomPanel`.
