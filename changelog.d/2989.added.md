- A backend-neutral record of the layouts painted through
  `DrawsPrimitives::draw_dyn` (#2989): every widget-contract widget with a
  `WidgetId` is recorded for the frame in
  `quadraui::primitive::PaintedWidgets` — absolute rect, downcastable
  layout, and the widget's own sub-part rects — queried through
  `PaintedWidgets::painted_layout` / `painted_hit` /
  `painted_sub_rect`/`painted_sub_center` (plus the thread-scoped
  `primitive::with_painted_widgets` / `painted_hit` / `painted_sub_center`
  entry points), and cleared at every backend's begin-frame. Widgets name
  their own sub-parts through the new defaulted
  `interactive::Interactive::sub_regions` (erased as
  `primitive::DynWidget::sub_regions`); `TabBar` implements it, with
  `primitives::tab_bar::{TabBarSubPart, tab_bar_sub_regions,
  tab_part_center}` encoding tab bodies, close buttons, scroll chevrons and
  right segments as `interactive::SubTarget` tags. `TuiDriver`/`GtkDriver`'s
  `tab_center`/`tab_close_center` and `compose::WorkspaceController`'s tab
  strip now resolve through the record, so a driver or binding layer can
  target one tab of a `draw_dyn`-painted bar without a per-backend layout
  cache.
