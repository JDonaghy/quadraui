- `AppShellEvent` is renamed `ShellEvent` and `AppShellLayout` is renamed
  `ShellLayout` (#2958), and `UiEvent::AppShell` is renamed
  `UiEvent::ShellView`. The binding wire key for that event changes from
  `"AppShell"` to `"ShellView"`; the payload shapes, the `app-shell:*` zone
  ids and the `bottom-panel:tabs`/`bp:maximise` widget ids are unchanged.
  `ShellContext::new` takes the frame's bottom-band bounds and hovered
  activity row, and `ShellContext::shell`/`shell_mut` lend the
  `ShellView` itself.
