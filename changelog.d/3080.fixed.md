  children instead of measuring without the padding and painting nothing,
  and a plain arrow key on a `List` or `Tree` moves the multi-selection's
  anchor to the cursor row, so a later Ctrl- or Shift-click grows the

  its own clicks and arrow keys (a click moves its cursor, the first Down

  than an empty path that pinned the cursor) (#3080).
