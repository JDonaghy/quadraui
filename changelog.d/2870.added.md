- `TextInput`, `TextField`, `CommandLine` and `TextDisplay` implement the
  widget contract (#2870): `Primitive` (layout, pixel paint and cell paint
  in the widget's own module) plus `Interactive`, so each can be pushed as
  a `Surface::Widget` and registered in a `FrameTree`. The three editable
  widgets share one editing core, `quadraui::behaviour::text_edit`, which
  `TextEditor` also routes through: `EditView` is the single `EditOp`
  mutator over `(lines, caret, anchor)`, `apply_flat_edit_op` its
  byte-offset bridge for a flat `String` value, and `flat_selection_range`
  the selected span a Ctrl-C needs. Keystrokes reach it through
  `EditOp::from_key`, which now also maps Ctrl-Left/Right and
  Ctrl-Backspace/Delete to the new `EditOp::MoveWordLeft`/`MoveWordRight`/
  `DeleteWordBackward`/`DeleteWordForward` word-motion variants; a mid-char
  byte cursor snaps to a char boundary instead of slicing, and a caret or
  selection anchor captured against longer content clamps into the live
  buffer — editing or copying a selection whose text the app has since
  shortened reads the text that is actually there instead of panicking.
  Edits ride
  `UiEvent::Form`'s `TextInputChanged`/`TextInputCommitted`,
  `TextDisplay`'s scrolling (arrows, PageUp/PageDown, Home/End, wheel,
  scrollbar track click and thumb drag) rides `UiEvent::TextDisplay`, and a
  copy or cut emits `UiEvent::TextCopied`.
  `quadraui::tui::{draw_text_input, draw_text_field, draw_text_display}`
  and their layout twins are thin delegates onto the widget-owned cell
  painters, which paint through `CellSurface`. The command line gets the
  same delegate pair, `draw_command_line{,_selection}`, but as a
  crate-internal rendering path rather than a reachable public one: a
  command line's selection highlight is now a parameter of that one
  painter rather than a second draw path.
