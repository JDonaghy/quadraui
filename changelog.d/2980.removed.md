- `CellSurface::ratatui_buffer` is gone (#2980): every cell painter writes
  through `CellSurface` verbs. Two verbs join the trait for decorating a
  cell another painter already filled: `set_bg` (replace its background)
  and `add_modifier` with the new `CellModifier` (reverse video, or an
  underline that keeps or recolours the cell's underline colour).
- The `quadraui::tui` cell rasterisers take `&mut dyn CellSurface` instead
  of a ratatui `Buffer`: `draw_list`, `draw_list_with_scroll_px`,
  `draw_list_with_state`, `draw_scrollbar`, `draw_focus_ring` (which now
  takes a `quadraui::Rect`), the `Toolbar` painters in
  `primitives::toolbar::cell_paint`, `primitives::form::cell_paint::draw_form`
  and `primitives::tab_bar::cell_paint::paint`. Wrap a bare buffer in
  `quadraui::tui::BufferCellSurface`.
