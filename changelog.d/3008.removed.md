- `Backend::draw_button`/`Backend::draw_button_with_state`/`Backend::button_layout`
  and the per-backend `tui`/`macos`/`win` `button` rasteriser wrapper files are
  removed (#3008): `Button` already paints and lays out through the widget
  contract (`Primitive` + `Interactive`, #3005), so every caller —
  `frame.rs`'s `OwnedContent::Button` arm, `primitives/dialog.rs`'s button-row
  rendering, `examples/common/{controls,grid}.rs`, the three
  `*_controls.rs` example runners, `quadraui-binding-core`'s band/leaf
  painters, and the `tui_focused_button_label.rs`, `macos_menu_reaction.rs`,
  `conformance/c0.rs` and `*_standalone_controls_driver.rs` test suites — now
  calls `DrawsPrimitives::draw_primitive`/`draw_dyn`/`layout_primitive`
  instead of a `Backend` method. The per-backend hover/pressed tint demo in
  `examples/common/controls.rs` and the `*_standalone_controls_driver.rs`
  suites is retired along with it, since no widget-contract paint entry
  point threads `is_hovered`/`is_pressed` yet; the shared pixel/cell painters
  that do still accept those flags keep direct unit coverage in
  `primitives/button.rs`. Neither removed `Backend` method had a caller in
  any published consumer.
