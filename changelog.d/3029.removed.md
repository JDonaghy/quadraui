- The last per-widget `Backend` methods are gone (#3029): `draw_list`,
  `draw_list_with_state`, `list_layout`, `list_vscrollbar`,
  `list_hscrollbar`, `list_char_width`, `draw_progress`,
  `progress_layout`, `draw_spinner`, `spinner_layout` and
  `draw_scrollbar`. Paint `ListView`, `ProgressBar`, `Spinner` and
  `Scrollbar` with `draw_dyn` (a `ListPaintState` or any `PaintState`
  carries `scroll_px`, striping and the focus-visible ring) and read
  geometry from `layout_primitive` — `ListViewPrimitiveLayout` reports
  `vscrollbar` and `hscrollbar`. On GTK, macOS and Windows a `ListView`
  painted through `draw_dyn` tints the row under the runtime's pointer as
  hovered or pressed, the same pointer feedback every other contract
  widget paints; `draw_list` painted no hover. The tint follows the
  runtime's `InteractionState`, so a list the pointer is not on paints
  exactly as before. A `Scrollbar` that must appear in the
  frame inventory registers its own zone (`Backend::register_zone`).
- `Surface::{List, Progress, Spinner, Scrollbar}` and
  `FrameZone::{List, Progress, Spinner, Scrollbar}` are gone (#3029): push
  the widget as a `Surface::Widget`. Captured `OwnedContent::{Progress,
  Spinner, Scrollbar}` stay and hit-test as `FrameZone::Widget`.
  A cancellable `ProgressBar` no longer gets a tab stop of its own.
- The per-backend widget painters are gone (#3029): `quadraui::tui::{draw_list,
  draw_list_with_state, draw_list_with_scroll_px, tui_list_layout,
  tui_list_layout_with_scroll_px, draw_progress, tui_progress_layout,
  draw_spinner, tui_spinner_layout, draw_scrollbar}`, the `quadraui::gtk`,
  `quadraui::macos` and `quadraui::win` `draw_*`/`*_layout` free
  functions for lists, progress bars, spinners, completions, dialogs,
  tooltips, toasts, text inputs, text displays, rich-text popups and the
  command line, and `quadraui::gtk::MenuOverlay`.
- `compose::ListHoverTracker` is gone (#3029): the runtime reports the
  hovered list row as a sub-part of the list's own id.
- `ContextMenu::move_selection(cur, ±1)` is gone (#3029); use
  `ContextMenu::navigate(cur, NavKey::Down | NavKey::Up, page).unwrap_or(cur)`,
  which also skips separators and disabled items and wraps at both ends. `PathPickerState::push_char`/`pop_char`
  are gone; set the query with `PathPickerState::set_query`.
- `layout_metrics::list_layout` is gone (#3029); use
  `ListView::resolve_layout`.
