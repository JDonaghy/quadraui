- `Dialog` and `ToastOverlay` implement the widget contract (#2868):
  `Primitive` (layout, pixel paint and cell paint in the widget's own
  module) plus `Interactive`, so either can be pushed as a
  `Surface::Widget` and registered in a `FrameTree`. `Dialog` declares one
  focus stop per button, cycles them with Tab/Shift-Tab, activates the
  focused button on Enter and cancels on Escape (`DialogAction`);
  `ToastOverlay` owns the whole stack's behaviour — click to dismiss,
  action-button click, and the keyboard model
  `compose::ToastStackController` now delegates to
  (`ToastOverlayAction`). `quadraui::tui::{draw_dialog, draw_toast_stack}`
  and their layout twins are thin delegates onto the widget-owned cell
  painters, which paint through `CellSurface`: dialog body/label text and
  toast titles now advance by display width, so a double-width glyph takes
  two columns instead of overlapping its neighbour. `Dialog`'s cell-units
  `Primitive::layout` resolves through one shared cell layout whatever
  backend features are compiled in, so a `DialogInput::Toolbar` body slot
  keeps its hit regions on a build with no terminal backend.
