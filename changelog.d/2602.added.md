- `text_util::fit_chars_to_cells` — how many leading characters of a string
  fit a terminal-cell budget, whether an ellipsis must follow them, and the
  cell width the result occupies. The shared display-width text fitting every
  cell-grid painter needs; `tui::data_table`'s body-cell painter is the first
  caller (#2602).
- `primitives::row_stack::stack_rows_from` — `layout_row_stack`'s stacking
  walk driven by a row *count* from an already-resolved scroll position,
  without the `scroll_offset` clamp or the last-row height clip. `DataTable`'s
  GUI and TUI painters stack their body rows through it (#2602).
