- macOS: a modal native service opened from `AppLogic::tick`/`handle`
  (`NSAlert` message/confirm dialogs, open/save panels, `NSMenu` popups) no
  longer panics with `RefCell already borrowed` when its nested run loop
  repaints the window or fires timers. The runner's paint/handle/tick
  closures, `mouseDown:` and `request_frame_in` now skip under borrow
  contention, and a skipped frame is repainted once the outer borrow ends
  (#3084, vimcode#2206).
