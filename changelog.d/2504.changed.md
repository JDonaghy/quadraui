- GTK's `TabBar` rasteriser (`gtk::tab_bar`) now paints through the shared
  `primitives::tab_bar::native_surface_paint::paint` the same way
  `gtk::toolbar`/`gtk::menu_bar`/`gtk::activity_bar` already do, instead of
  its own Cairo per-tab paint loop; its boundary-tab ellipsis-fit geometry
  (`pixel_tab_bar_layout_fitting_boundary`) moves next to its only other
  callers, `GtkBackend`'s no-paint `tab_bar_layout*`/`resolve_tab_bar_layout*`
  twins, in `gtk::backend` (#2504). `TabFrame::Brackets` framing now paints
  through the shared painter too (`native_surface_paint::paint` gained a
  `chrome: &TabChrome` parameter for this). A handful of small visual
  deltas come with the migration: the active chip, inactive fill and focus
  ring now span the tab's full slot width including `TAB_OUTER_GAP` (up to
  1px wider, adjacent tabs touch); the close/dirty glyph centres inside its
  hit region instead of starting a fixed leading gap after it; the
  close-hover backdrop now covers the whole hit region rather than just the
  glyph's own width plus a small pad; and the label paints in the ambient
  font with the Nerd Font fallback applied, matching every other
  `native_surface_paint`-painted primitive.
