- `PasswordInput`, `TextArea` and `Markdown`: core widgets on the widget
  contract. `PasswordInput` and `TextArea` paint through the form's own
  per-kind control painter and edit through the shared flat-value editing
  handler (a password never copies or cuts its value out). `Markdown`
  renders its source through `render_markdown_to_styled` into a composed
  `TextDisplay`: each link line is a sub-region and a press on it reports
  `UiEvent::RichTextLinkClicked`; the wheel scrolls through the display's
  own `TextDisplayEvent::Scrolled`.
