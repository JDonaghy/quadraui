- `Sidebar` is a composite widget on the widget contract (#2953): it is
  laid out and painted as a `MultiSectionView`, each section body a
  `TreeView` or `Form` painted and driven through its own widget. It is
  controlled: collapse, visibility, the active section, each tree's
  selection and scroll offset and the panel scroll are descriptor
  fields, and gestures report `SidebarAction`s (`Sidebar::apply` stores
  them). New `UiEvent::Sidebar(WidgetId, SidebarAction)`, `SidebarBody`,
  `SidebarSection`, `SidebarLayout` and `SidebarState`.
  `SidebarSystem` keeps its API, drives a `Sidebar` underneath and adds
  `rows(section)` and `set_empty_space_context_menu(section, enabled)`.
