- `quadraui::flex` (`FlexLayout`, `pack_1d` and the re-exported `taffy`
  styles), the `layout` Cargo feature and the `taffy` dependency (#2976).
  `ShellView`, `Form` and `Toolbar` pack their items with the same
  arithmetic they used without the feature, so their geometry is
  unchanged. Use `quadraui::layout::stack` (stacked rows/columns) or
  `quadraui::layout::resolve_grid_tracks` (2D grids) instead.
