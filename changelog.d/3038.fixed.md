- Widgets painted through `draw_dyn` on GTK/macOS/Win now look, lay out and
  hit-test like the legacy `Backend::draw_*` methods they replace (#3038): a
  `bordered` `ListView` clips its content to a rounded rect, strokes its
  1px `border_fg` frame and insets (and hit-tests) its rows inside it; a
  contract `Scrollbar` paints at the backend's overlay idle-fade opacity
  (hidden until the user scrolls, full while hovered or dragged); a
  `DataTable` `Content` column measures its title in the bold chrome font
  the header paints it in, so the title is no longer ellipsised inside the
  column it sized; a `TreeView`'s pixel layout reads the host's live
  `Style` (`row_padding_x`, `scrollbar_width`) and measures its chevrons
  through the backend; and `ListView` takes hover and press for a row from
  the runtime's pointer state, as `TreeView` and `DataTable` do. The
  find/replace panel paints with the backend's own `Style` instead of
  `Style::default()`, so a host resolves its click geometry with
  `FindReplaceMetrics::resolve(panel, &backend.style(), …)`.
