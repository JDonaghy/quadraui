- `TabGroupController` is a thin wrapper over `TabGroup` (#2955): it
  paints through the widget contract and routes its `handle_*` calls
  through `TabGroup`'s `Interactive::handle`. Its dividers paint as one
  `SplitTree`, so a stacked divider meeting a side-by-side one draws a
  junction glyph (`├ ┤ ┬ ┴ ┼`). A divider drag places the divider under
  the pointer (the ratio now accounts for the divider's own thickness)
  and clamps ratios to `SplitTree`'s `0.1..=0.9`. A stacked pane is
  never squeezed shorter than its tab strip. A pane's strip scrolls the
  active tab into view on the frame it becomes active.
- A drop zone on a pane less than six units tall splits into its top and
  bottom edges instead of panicking (#2955).
- **Breaking:** `TabGroupEvent` gains `TabDropped` and
  `DividerResized` gains `ratio`; `TabGroupEvent` and
  `TabDropInstruction` derive `Serialize`/`Deserialize`.
  `TabGroupController::layout` returns `GroupLayout` by value. `Pane` and
  `PaneTab` are aliases of `TabGroupPane` / `TabGroupTab` over
  `Box<dyn BackendWidget>`.
