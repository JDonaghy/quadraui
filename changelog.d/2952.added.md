- `AppMenu` is the menu system on the widget contract (#2952): the bar
  paints and routes through `MenuBar`'s contract impls and each open
  dropdown level is a `ContextMenu` popup the widget owns through
  `Interactive::popups`, with submenus as nested popups. Its view
  (`AppMenuView`) reaches a runtime host as the new `UiEvent::AppMenu`;
  activations ride `UiEvent::MenuActivated`. `AppMenu` and `MenuDef`
  serialise with the `menu_bar` leaf shape.
