- `SidebarPanelBody` implements `Primitive` and is `non_interactive`
  (#2953). `render`/`render_with` paint the panel through `draw_dyn`: the
  background and header/search chrome no longer go through
  `Backend::draw_solid_fill`/`Backend::draw_settings_chrome`, and the
  `Search`/`StatusBars` rows paint through `StatusBar`'s own `Primitive`
  inside the panel, so they are no longer separate entries in the
  painted-widget record. Chrome rows stay pitched at the backend's chrome
  line height. New `SidebarPanelBodyPrimitiveLayout`.
