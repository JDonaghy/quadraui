- `quadraui::ToolbarButton::Field`/`ToolbarButton::MenuButton` (#2595):
  `Toolbar` can now host an embedded `TextField` search box (`Field`,
  sized `ToolbarItemWidth::Fixed(px_or_cells)` or
  `ToolbarItemWidth::Flex { min, max }` so it grows into spare bar room)
  and a pull-down button (`MenuButton`, painted as a pill plus a trailing
  `▾`, hit-testing to `ToolbarHit::MenuButton` with its own anchor rect in
  `ToolbarLayout::visible_items`) — the Finder-style toolbar shape. New
  `ToolbarEvent::FieldChanged`/`FieldCommitted`/`FieldCleared` variants
  carry a `Field`'s edits; a `Field` is never dropped into a
  `ToolbarOverflow::Menu` overflow menu, shrinking toward its own minimum
  and, past that, painting icon-only instead.
