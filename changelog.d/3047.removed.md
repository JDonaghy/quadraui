- `FilePickerController`, `FolderPickerController` and their deprecated
  `FilePickerEvent`/`FolderPickerEvent` are gone (#3047, #2957). Host the
  picker widget itself: `FilePicker`/`FolderPicker` are now aliases of one
  generic `PathPicker<K: PickerKind>` (`FileKind`/`FolderKind`). Paint it
  with `draw_dyn` or a `Surface::Widget`; on the routing engine its
  actions arrive as `UiEvent::PathPicker`, and a host reading raw events
  calls `PathPicker::handle_event` and hands each browsing change to
  `PathPicker::apply`. `FilePickerState`/`FolderPickerState` are replaced
  by `PathPickerEditState`.
