- `Switch`, `RadioGroup` and `Button` implement the widget contract (#3005):
  `Primitive` (measure, layout, pixel paint through each module's existing
  `native_surface_paint`, and cell paint through a new `cell_paint` module
  moved out of `quadraui::tui::{switch,radio_group,button}`) plus
  `Interactive`. `Switch` and `Button` are each one keyboard focus stop
  (`Enter`/`Space` activates, a left click activates and requests focus);
  `RadioGroup` declares `FocusSpec::Children`, one stop per option, with
  arrow keys moving the cursor and a click selecting the row under the
  pointer. All three can now be pushed as a `Surface::Widget` and
  registered in a `FrameTree`; `quadraui::tui::{draw_switch,
  draw_radio_group, draw_button}` are now thin delegates onto the
  widget-owned cell painters, which paint through `CellSurface`.
  `OwnedContent` gains `Switch`/`RadioGroup`/`Button` capture variants, and
  `widget_kinds!` gains `"switch"`/`"radio_group"`/`"button"` entries so
  `decode_widget` can decode all three from the wire. `UiEvent` gains
  `SwitchToggled`, `RadioGroupSelectionChanged` and `ButtonActivated` for
  the events each widget's `Interactive::handle` now produces.
  `ButtonLayout` gains a `nerd_fonts_enabled` field, carried from
  `LayoutCtx` at layout time so `paint_cells` (which takes no `LayoutCtx`
  of its own) still knows whether to paint a Nerd Font icon glyph or its
  ASCII fallback — the same convention `TabBarLayout::nerd_fonts_enabled`
  and `ActivityBarLayout::nerd_fonts` already follow.

  This slice only adds the contract impls; it does not remove any
  existing `Backend::{draw_switch, switch_layout, draw_radio_group,
  radio_group_layout, draw_button, draw_button_with_state,
  button_layout}` method, per-backend wrapper file, or binding-core
  dispatch. Those seven methods are recorded in
  `KNOWN_LEGACY_PRIMITIVE_BACKEND_METHODS` as debt the sibling retirement
  issues pay off, one widget at a time.
