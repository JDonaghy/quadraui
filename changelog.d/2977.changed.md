- The `"menu_bar"` wire kind decodes to `AppMenu`, the whole application
  menu with its dropdown cascade as popups, instead of the bare `MenuBar`
  strip (#2977, 0.12 rec 12). `AppMenu` and `MenuDef` derive `JsonSchema`
  under the `schema` feature.
- `Form`'s widget contract reads its live state (#2977): its layout and
  paint apply the stored `FormState`'s scroll offset, caret and field
  focus, so a wheel notch or a scroll-into-view moves what is painted and
  hit-tested; a host reporting the form focused through
  `PaintState::focus_ring_visible` shows its focused-row highlight. Its
  `Interactive::handle` requests focus for the newly focused field's stop
  (`FocusSpec::Children`'s sub-target) whenever field focus moves, so a
  host knows which child holds focus; moves field focus on a plain Up/Down
  the focused field has no use for, stopping at either end; and captures
  the pointer for a slider drag. `FormDialog`'s Tab hand-off reads the
  form's live field focus, so it follows focus the form tracks itself.
