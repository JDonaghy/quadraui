- Every editable text widget undoes and redoes through one shared history
  (#2978 TE7): `behaviour::text_edit::EditHistory` serves `EditOp::Undo`/
  `EditOp::Redo` for `TextEditor`, `LinesEditState` (`TextInput`) and
  `FlatEditState` (`TextField`, `CommandLine`, the palette query,
  `PasswordInput`, `TextArea`, ...). Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes and Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z
  redoes in every one of them; with nothing to give back the chord stays
  `Ignored`, so an app accelerator still fires. The history survives a
  focus change. **Breaking:** `FlatEditState` and `LinesEditState` gain a
  `history` field and are no longer `Copy`, and neither are
  `DualModePaletteState` and `PathPickerEditState`, which hold one.
- `TextInput` paints the caret its own `TextInputState` walked to, not the
  descriptor's `cursor_line`/`cursor_col` (#2978 TE11), so a host that
  never echoes a caret back still sees it where typing lands.
  `TextInputPrimitiveLayout` gains `line_height`, `units` and `relayout`.
- `"text_input"` (`TextInput`) and `"text_field"` (`TextField`) join the
  static `widget_kinds!` table.
- `quadraui-binding-core`: `"text_input"`, `"text_field"`, `"password"` and
  `"textarea"` are generic `LeafDescriptor::Widget` leaves decoded through
  `widget_kinds!` and edited through the core widgets' own `Interactive`
  state; their `ExtraPaint` impls, the binding-side `PasswordInput`/
  `TextArea` wrappers, `extra_as_text_input` and `text_input.rs`
  (`TextEditorCache`, `text_input_key_event`, `text_input_insert_event`,
  `text_input_click_event`, `apply_text_input_edit`, `clamp_cursor`) are
  deleted, along with `InteractionEngine::sync_text_input_carets`.
  `InteractionEngine::paint_with_widget_state(paint)` installs the
  engine's per-widget state for a paint pass, so every generic leaf paints
  from the state its own input handling walked; that state is keyed by the
  widget's own id (else the leaf id). A dialog's or popover's layout
  subtree routes keys and committed text to a focused generic leaf
  through the same dispatch.
