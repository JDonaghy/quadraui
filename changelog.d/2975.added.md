- `widget_kinds!` decodes `message_list`, `password`, `textarea`,
  `data_table` and `multi_section_view` (#2975).
- `FindReplacePanel` is a contract widget (#2975): it fills the rect it
  is laid out in (`FindReplaceLayout`), carries an `id` (default
  `"find_replace"`), and its `Interactive` reports
  `UiEvent::FindReplace(id, FindReplaceEvent)` in controlled mode —
  field edits through the shared flat text-edit core, Tab between the
  fields, Enter/Shift+Enter for next/previous match, Enter/Ctrl+Enter
  in the replace field for replace/replace all, Alt+C/W/R/P and clicks
  for the switches, Escape to close. `FindReplacePanel::apply_event`
  writes an event back; `anchored_rect` places the box at the top-right
  of `group_bounds`. It is registered as the `find_replace` widget kind.
