- `primitive.rs` gains `Units`, `LayoutMeasure`, `PrimitiveLayout<L>`, `SubTarget`,
  `PaintTarget`, `PaintServices`, `PaintEnv` and the `PaintState` trait, plus
  `LayoutCtx::resolve_row_height`/`LayoutCtx::measure` (#2851) — pure
  additions laying the groundwork for a future widget contract; no existing
  `Primitive` implementor changes shape. `DrawsPrimitives::draw_primitive`
  is tightened to match: it floors the `LayoutCtx` it builds at the active
  `Style::row_height` via `LayoutCtx::resolve_row_height` (matching every
  bespoke `draw_list`/`draw_tree`/`draw_data_table` path's own row-pitch
  resolution), clips a `PaintSurface`-backed paint to the primitive's `rect`,
  and panics with a diagnostic instead of silently skipping a TUI
  `Primitive::paint_cells` call made outside a frame scope.
