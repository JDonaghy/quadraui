- `Button::hovered`/`pressed`, `Switch::hovered`/`pressed`,
  `RadioOption::hovered`/`pressed` and `ActivityBar::hovered`, with the
  matching binding wire-descriptor keys (#3024): hover and press reach
  these widgets' painters through `PaintEnv`. Pass an `InteractionState`
  as the `draw_dyn` state to pin them, or `&()` for the ambient pointer
  state. Binding-core's `InteractionEngine::chrome_interaction`,
  `hit::chrome_hover_hit` and the `interaction` parameter of
  `ExtraPaint::paint_chrome`/`draw_bands` are removed with them; the
  engine feeds the ambient state instead.
