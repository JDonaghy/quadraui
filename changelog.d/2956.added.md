- `Workspace` is a widget-contract descriptor (#2956): its strip paints
  and routes through a `TabBar` child sharing its id, and a click reports
  `UiEvent::Workspace` activate / close / promote intents (controlled
  mode), with the strip's scroll offset derived in layout.
  `WorkspaceController` now owns one and forwards to it.
- `ShellApp::frame_tree` lets a shell-hosted app route its own widgets
  through the runtime, the `AppLogic::frame_tree` counterpart (#2957); the
  gallery's `Demo::frame_tree` forwards to it for the Demo tab.
