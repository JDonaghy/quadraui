- Widget-owned popups and persistent per-widget state (#3028). A widget
  answers `Interactive::popups(&self, state, layout) -> Vec<Popup>`
  (default none) with the popups it has open; `Popup::new(key, anchor,
  size, content)` (plus `side`, `z` and `dismiss`) anchors a content widget
  in the owner's local space. Open popups paint above the whole frame
  (each runner calls `paint_open_popups` after `render`), are hit-tested
  before every node (`FrameTree::hit`, `FrameHit`, `push_popup`,
  `popup_at`), and route to their owner as `RoutedEvent::PopupEvent { popup,
  event }`; an outside press or Escape arrives as
  `RoutedEvent::PopupDismissed { popup, reason }`. `popup::painted_popups`
  reports what painted. `PaintEnv::widget_state` (read with
  `widget_state_as::<T>()`) hands a widget its persistent
  `Interactive::State` at paint, and `Primitive::native_presentation` lets
  a widget present itself through the platform instead of painting —
  `MenuBar` uses it for the native macOS menu bar.
- `DatePicker` can present as a dropdown (#3028): with `dropdown: true` it
  paints a one-row date field, and while the app's `open` is set its
  calendar is a widget-owned popup that dismisses on an outside press or
  Escape. The new `DatePickerEvent::OpenChanged(bool)` reaches the app as
  `FormEvent::DateOpenChanged { id, open }`; `DateSelected` also closes the
  popup. Both descriptor fields default to `false` on the wire, so an
  inline calendar is unchanged. Day cells under the pointer now paint a
  hover wash.
