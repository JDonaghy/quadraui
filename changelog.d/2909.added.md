- Every backend now keeps a `focus::FocusTree` alongside its existing
  `focus::FocusManager`, so the widget-contract routing engine's
  `EventHost::is_focused`/`request_focus` can finally tell a composite
  widget's own children apart by `SubTarget` instead of answering every
  child identically for one focused id. `set_focus` keeps both in step for
  every whole-widget focus change, and the shared runner's Tab/Shift+Tab
  cycling keeps them in step too. `FocusTree` derives
  `Debug`/`Clone`/`PartialEq`, matching `FocusManager`.
  `AppLogic::extra_tab_stops` is a new, empty-by-default hook alongside
  `AppLogic::tab_stops` (the shared runner reads and cycles both,
  concatenated) for an app that wants a second source of Tab stops
  without touching its existing override.
