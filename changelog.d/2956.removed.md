- **Breaking:** `WorkspaceController` and its `WorkspaceLayout` are
  removed (#2957). `Workspace` is the model: `open`, `open_preview`,
  `close`, `activate`, `activate_index`, `reorder`, `cycle`, `promote`,
  `set_label` and `set_dirty` move onto it unchanged, and
  `Workspace::apply` writes back the `UiEvent::Workspace` intents its
  routed strip reports (an `Activated` intent never promotes; a click on
  the preview tab reports `Promoted` separately). A host binds
  `Ctrl+Tab`-style cycling app-wide with `Workspace::cycle_delta` +
  `Workspace::cycle`.
