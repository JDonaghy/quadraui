- `TreeView`'s `Primitive::layout` reserves a one-column scrollbar on a
  cell grid when the rows overflow, and paints it through `Scrollbar`'s
  own `Primitive` (#2947). `TreeViewPrimitiveLayout` gains `row_pitch`,
  `viewport_rows`, `vscrollbar` and `vscrollbar_layout`; on a cell grid
  its `area` excludes the scrollbar column. `TreeView` now reports its
  `id`, so pixel paint tints the hovered and pressed row from the
  runtime's hover channel.
- `TreeEvent` no longer derives `Eq` (`ContextMenuRequested` carries a
  `Point`).
- `TreeController`'s rename session edits through the shared text editor,
  so it also gains word motion and word delete (Ctrl+Left/Right,
  Ctrl+Backspace/Delete).
