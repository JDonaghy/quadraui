- GTK: the `quadraui` lib test binary could intermittently crash with
  `SIGSEGV` mid-run on `gtk (build, test, clippy)` CI (#2303, follow-up to
  #2284/#2296). A dozen `GtkBackend` measurement methods
  (`editor_pango_layout`, `create_stable_pango_layout`,
  `chrome_measure_layout`, `measure_text`, `status_bar_layout`,
  `tab_bar_layout_icons`/`resolve_tab_bar_layout_icons`,
  `tab_bar_layout_with_chrome`/`resolve_tab_bar_layout_with_chrome`,
  `menu_bar_layout`, `toolbar_layout`/`toolbar_layout_with_overflow`,
  `sidebar_panel_layout`) can run outside `GtkBackend::enter_frame_scope`
  (click-time hit-testing with no live frame) and touch the stored Pango
  context directly without serialising against `TEST_RENDER_LOCK`, so two
  such calls on different libtest threads could race Pango's
  process-global font map. They now take the same re-entrant
  `#[cfg(test)]` guard `enter_frame_scope` does.
