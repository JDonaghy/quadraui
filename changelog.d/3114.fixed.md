- Python and Node now match Rust on two row-widget inputs, since both go
  through the core widgets: Down on a focused `List` whose selection the app
  pins moves the selection and reports `SelectionChanged` (it used to do
  nothing), and Ctrl+Space or Insert on a `"Multi"` table toggles the row
  the last click moved the cursor to, not the app-echoed `selected_idx`.
