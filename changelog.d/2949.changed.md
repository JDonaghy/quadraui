- The chat input edits through the shared text-edit core (#2949), like
  every other text widget: word motion and word delete stop at
  punctuation (letters, digits and `_` make a word), `Ctrl+A` selects
  the whole input instead of moving to the line start, `Ctrl+C`/`Ctrl+X`
  copy and cut a selection, and a drag in the input selects. `Ctrl+E` is
  no longer a line-end key; it reaches the app as `KeyPressed`.
- The chat's Send/Stop segment is a `Button` rather than a one-field
  `Form`, and its parts paint through their own widgets' painters, so
  the segment and (on GTK) the transcript rows look slightly different.
- `ChatController::set_transcript_scroll_top` stops following the
  transcript's tail.
