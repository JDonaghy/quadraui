- `quadraui::layout::stack`, the one stack layout solver (#2976):
  `resolve_main_axis_sizes` (the `Fixed`/`Content` → `Percent` →
  `Weight`/`EqualShare` pass order over `SectionSize`), `justify_starts`,
  `align_cross`, `stack_rects` (a whole row/column, including `wrap`),
  `split_rects`/`split_rects_with_gap`, `shrink_main_axis`/
  `apply_split_gap`, and the `quantise_*` native-unit helpers. `Justify`,
  `Align`, `EdgeInsets` and `Spacing` are re-exported from the crate root.

  a section's header chrome cost. `quadraui::layout::grid_cell_rects`
  resolves a spaced grid's cells row-major.
