- `Terminal` is a widget-contract widget (#2827): it implements `Primitive`
  and `Interactive`, reaches a frame only as `Surface::Widget`, and decodes
  through `widget_kinds!` as `"terminal"`. Its layout reserves the scrollbar
  gutter (`TerminalScrollbar::width`, else one cell on TUI and
  `Style::scrollbar_width` on a pixel backend, which replaces the fixed
  8px) before dividing the pane into editor-font cells, so
  `TerminalLayout::hit_test`/`cell_bounds` match what paint drew;
  `TerminalLayout` gains `area` and `scrollbar`. A left press on a cell
  focuses the terminal and emits `TerminalEvent::SelectStart`, a drag
  `SelectExtend`, the release `SelectEnd`, and a wheel notch `Scroll`, all as
  `UiEvent::Terminal`; keys still bubble to the host. The split-pane divider
  is the new `TerminalDivider` primitive. `Backend::draw_terminal`,
  `Backend::terminal_layout`, `Backend::terminal_scrollbar_default_width`,
  `Backend::draw_terminal_divider`, `quadraui::tui::draw_terminal`,
  `quadraui::tui::draw_terminal_divider`, `Surface::Terminal` and
  `FrameZone::Terminal` are removed: paint a terminal with
  `DrawsPrimitives::draw_dyn`/`draw_primitive` (or push a
  `Surface::Widget`) and resolve its geometry with
  `DrawsPrimitives::layout_primitive`. `LayoutCtx` gains
  `editor_line_height`, `PaintSurface` gains `surface_with_editor_font`, and
  `CellSurface` gains `put_grapheme`. In the bindings a click on a
  `"terminal"` leaf now arrives as a `Terminal` `SelectStart` event instead
  of a raw `MouseDown`; the wire shape of the descriptor is unchanged.
