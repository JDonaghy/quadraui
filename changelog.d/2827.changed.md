- `SidebarPanel` is on the widget contract (#2827): it implements `Primitive`
  and `Interactive`, reaches a frame as `Surface::Widget`, and decodes through
  `widget_kinds!` as `"sidebar_panel"`. `Primitive::layout` resolves a
  `SidebarPanelLayout` whose panel/header/content rects are absolute and
  whose `toolbar` field is the header's own `ToolbarPrimitiveLayout`
  (header-local item bounds, replacing the old absolute `toolbar_layout`);
  the default header height is one cell on a cell grid and
  `Style::toolbar_height` (floored at the row height) on a pixel surface.
  `Interactive` is pointer-only (`FocusSpec::None`): hover, press and click on
  a header button go through the header `Toolbar`'s own `Interactive` impl
  and arrive as `UiEvent::Toolbar(toolbar_id, ..)`, and an assistive-technology
  `Click` activates the first enabled header button. Breaking:
  `Backend::draw_sidebar_panel_interactive`/`Backend::sidebar_panel_layout`,
  `Surface::SidebarPanel`, `FrameZone::SidebarPanel` (a captured panel now
  reports `FrameZone::Widget`), the inherent `SidebarPanel::layout` and
  `SidebarPanelMeasure`, the `tui`/`gtk`/`macos`/`win` `sidebar_panel`
  rasteriser modules (`draw_sidebar_panel`, `tui_sidebar_panel_layout`,
  `gtk_sidebar_panel_layout`, `mac_sidebar_panel_layout`,
  `win_sidebar_panel_layout`) are removed — paint with
  `DrawsPrimitives::draw_dyn`/`draw_primitive` and hit-test with
  `layout_primitive`. A `SidebarPanel` is no longer a `ScreenLayout` tab stop.
  In `quadraui-binding-core`, the `"sidebar_panel"` layout node's header is
  recorded as a generic `PaintedHit::Widget`, so `PaintedHit::SidebarPanel`
  and the `sidebar_panel_toolbar_button` hit-detail kind are removed from the
  wire; a header-button click still emits the same `Toolbar` event.
