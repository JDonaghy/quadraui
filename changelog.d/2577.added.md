- `TuiBackend::draw_image` now paints a `▀`-glyph half-block mosaic on a
  truecolor terminal that hasn't confirmed Kitty-protocol graphics support
  (#2577): `tui::image::draw_image_halfblock` decodes through
  `tui::image_decode` and downscales to `(cols × 2·rows)` samples, filling
  each cell's foreground with its top sample and background with its
  bottom sample (alpha-blended onto the theme background), and reports
  `ImagePaintResult::Painted`. A 256/16-colour terminal or a real decode
  failure still falls all the way back to `Image::fallback_text` and
  `ImagePaintResult::Unsupported`, the same as before.
