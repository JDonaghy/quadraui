- `MessageDialogController`, `FormDialogController` and
  `HelpOverlayController` are thin wrappers over the new widgets (#2950).
  `FormDialogController` no longer embeds a `FormController`: its form
  is laid out, painted and edited through `Form`'s own contract impls.
  The form keeps its vertical scrollbar and keyboard item-focus ring,
  painted through `Scrollbar`'s `Primitive` and the focus-ring chrome;
  `FormDialogLayout` gains `scrollbar` and `line_height`.
