- A dragged split divider lands exactly under the pointer (#2968):
  `SplitTreeDivider::ratio_at` inverts the layout over the span minus the
  divider's thickness, and `dispatch_mouse_drag` and the binding layer's
  split drags use it. `DragTarget::SplitDivider` carries the grabbed
  `divider: SplitTreeDivider` in place of `split_index`, `direction`,
  `axis_start` and `axis_size`.
- On a cell grid a `SplitTree` places every divider on the whole cell it
  paints at and lays its sub-trees out against that cell, so a leaf rect
  never covers a divider (#2968); `tui_split_tree_layout` returns these
  rects.
