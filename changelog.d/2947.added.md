- `TreeView` implements `Interactive` (#2947): arrow/Home/End/Page
  navigation (plus `j`/`k` when the new `TreeStyle::vim_keys` is set),
  Left/Right collapse-or-parent and expand-or-first-child,
  Space and chevron clicks toggle a branch, Enter activates, typing
  letters jumps to the first matching row, the wheel and a scrollbar
  thumb drag scroll, Ctrl/Shift-click grows a multi-selection, and an
  open rename session (`TreeRow::edit`) edits through the shared text
  editor. Selection, scroll offset and rename text stay app-owned:
  `TreeViewState` holds only gesture data. New `TreeViewSubPart` names
  each row, chevron and the scrollbar for hover and drivers.
- `TreeEvent::RowActivated`, `TreeEvent::ScrollChanged` and
  `TreeEvent::ContextMenuRequested`, plus the shared rename helpers
  `tree_rename_key`/`tree_rename_insert` and `TreeRenameKey`.
