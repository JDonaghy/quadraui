- Bindings: the `checkbox`, `slider` and `select` layout kinds decode into
  the core widgets through the generic widget seam (`WIDGET_KINDS`)
  instead of binding-only extras. Their wire shapes and events are
  unchanged. A standalone slider press now maps across its painted track
  rather than the whole leaf width.
- A `Form` `Slider` row now also steps ten steps on PageUp/PageDown, the
  same stepping the standalone `Slider` uses.
