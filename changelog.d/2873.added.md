- `MenuBar` and `ContextMenu` now implement the widget contract
  (`Primitive` + `Interactive`) in their own modules (#2873), so each one
  reaches a frame type-erased as `Surface::Widget`, sits in a
  `FrameTree`, and routes its own input instead of needing a per-backend
  `draw_*`/`*_layout` pair:
  - `MenuBar::pixel_layout`/`MenuBar::cell_layout` are the two layout
    derivations every backend now measures through — GTK's Pango twin,
    macOS's Core Text twin, Windows' DirectWrite twin and TUI's cell-grid
    twin each pass their own measurer and share `ITEM_H_PADDING`, and
    `display_text`/`alt_char_index`/`cell_item_width` are public
    helpers rather than four private copies.
  - `MenuBarEvent` reports a strip's outcomes: `FocusMoved` for a
    keyboard/pointer cursor move, `MenuOpened` for a menu asking to open
    (as `UiEvent::MenuActivated` under the top-level item's own id) and
    `Closed` for Escape. Left/Right walk the menus (wrapping, skipping
    disabled ones, carrying an open menu along), Down/Enter open the
    focused one, F10 enters the strip, Alt+mnemonic opens a menu
    directly, and a click toggles a menu open or closed.
  - `ContextMenuEvent` reports a popup's outcomes: `SelectionChanged`,
    `SubmenuOpened`, `Activated` (as `UiEvent::ContextMenuItemActivated`)
    and `Dismissed` (as `UiEvent::ContextMenuDismissed`). Up/Down (and
    Home/End/PageUp/PageDown) move the highlight through
    `behaviour::nav::FlatNav`, skipping separators and disabled items;
    typing jumps it to the best prefix match through
    `behaviour::typeahead::Typeahead`; Enter/Right open a submenu parent;
    Left/Escape close; and a click activates. `ContextMenuController`
    resolves every keystroke and click through those shared rules instead
    of keeping its own copy.
  - `ContextMenuPrimitiveLayout` reports the box's `border_inset`, the
    `viewport` the items were placed in and the host's
    `nerd_fonts_enabled` flag, so a caller reads the border reservation
    off the resolved layout and both paint paths resolve an icon against
    the same font face; `ContextMenu::item_heights` is the one
    item/separator row-height rule both menu styles use.
  - Both widgets' TUI rasterisers moved into the widget modules as
    `cell_paint`, written against `CellSurface`; `quadraui::tui`'s
    `draw_menu_bar`/`draw_context_menu*` are thin `Buffer` →
    `CellSurface` delegates onto them, and paint identically.
