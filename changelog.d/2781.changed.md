- `AppShellEvent` is now `#[non_exhaustive]` (#2781): a downstream `match`
  on `quadraui::compose::app_shell::AppShellEvent` must add a wildcard arm
  (`_ => {}`) to keep compiling. Construction
  (`AppShellEvent::PanelChanged { panel_id }`) and single-variant
  destructuring (`if let AppShellEvent::SidebarHidden = ev`) are
  unaffected. This closes off a future variant (e.g. a new panel-band
  event) from being a breaking change on its own.
  In-repo blast radius: every exhaustive `AppShellEvent` match inside
  `quadraui/src` (non_exhaustive doesn't constrain the defining crate's
  own matches) needs no change; the one exhaustive in-tree match living
  outside the defining crate (`examples/common/shell_app.rs`, a separate
  binary crate) now has a wildcard arm added. Grepping `vimcode` and
  `coord-tui` found every exhaustive `AppShellEvent` match already falls
  through a final wildcard/catch-all arm, so neither needs a migration.
