- `quadraui::tui::image_decode` (#2574, behind the new opt-in
  `tui-images` Cargo feature) and `quadraui::tui::kitty_graphics`
  (behind the bare `tui` feature): decode + downscale raster images
  (PNG/JPEG/GIF first-frame/WebP, via the `image` crate) and turn them
  into Kitty graphics protocol bytes — transmit (RGBA or PNG
  passthrough, chunked), Unicode-placeholder virtual placement, delete,
  and placeholder cell content/foreground-colour encoding. Both modules
  are self-contained and not yet wired into any paint path.
