- `ShellView` and `BottomPanel` are widget-contract descriptors (#2954):
  `Primitive` + `Interactive` for the app shell and the tabbed bottom
  panel, composed from `ActivityBar`, `StatusBar` and `TabBar` children,
  reporting `UiEvent::AppShell` / `UiEvent::BottomPanel` intents
  (controlled mode). `AppShell` and `BottomPanelController` now own one
  of these and forward to it; the `bottom-panel:tabs` / `bp:maximise` ids
  are unchanged.
- `Interactive::sub_region_name` lets `draw_dyn` publish a widget's named
  sub-regions as frame-inventory zones `"<id>:<name>"`, so `ShellView`
  still reports every `app-shell:*` chrome zone; `Interactive::
  HOVER_ON_SUB_REGIONS_ONLY` keeps a window-sized composite from being a
  hover target outside the parts that react to the pointer.
- `LayoutCtx::chrome_line_height` exposes the backend's chrome line
  height before the row-height floor; `AppShellEvent::ActivityCursorMoved`
  reports keyboard cursor moves on the shell's activity bar.
