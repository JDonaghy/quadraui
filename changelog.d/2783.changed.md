- `MenuEvent` is now `#[non_exhaustive]` (#2783): a downstream `match` on
  `quadraui::compose::menu_system::MenuEvent` must add a wildcard arm
  (`_ => {}`) to keep compiling. Construction (`MenuEvent::Activated(id)`)
  and single-variant destructuring (`if let MenuEvent::Ignored = ev`) are
  unaffected. This closes off a future variant (e.g. a highlight-only
  event distinct from `Consumed`) from being a breaking change on its own.
  In-repo blast radius: `MenuSystem`'s own matches inside
  `quadraui/src/compose/menu_system.rs` needed no change (non_exhaustive
  doesn't constrain the defining crate's own matches). Five exhaustive
  matches living outside the defining crate now have a wildcard arm added:
  `quadraui-binding-core`'s `engine.rs` dispatch, `quadraui-gallery`'s
  `menu_bar.rs` demo, and the TUI examples `menu_bar_app.rs`,
  `shell_menu_demo.rs` and `submenu_app.rs`. Grepping `coord-tui` found no
  `MenuEvent` usage. `vimcode`'s `app/dispatch.rs` has one exhaustive
  `MenuEvent` match with no wildcard arm and needs the same one-line
  migration before it next updates past this release.
