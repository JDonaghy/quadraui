- The macOS and Windows editors paint through the editor's shared pixel
  painter (#2962), so they gain the features GTK already had: diagnostic
  and spell underlines, indent guides, colour columns, bracket match, AI
  ghost text, secondary carets, breakpoint/git gutter columns and the
  diagnostic dot / lightbulb. The block caret is now a translucent cell
  over the glyph (`cursor_normal_alpha`), and Win positions text by
  DirectWrite advances rather than a fixed cell grid.
