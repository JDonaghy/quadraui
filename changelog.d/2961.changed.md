- The GTK editor paints through the editor's own shared pixel painter
  (#2961), built from GTK's pass structure on `PaintSurface` alone:
  `gtk::draw_editor*` are thin delegates, and `Surface::Widget` editors get
  the full painter (gutter, diagnostics, spell and diagnostic underlines,
  indent guides, colour columns, bracket match, ghost text, secondary
  carets) on every pixel backend. The caret is always visible. GTK output
  is pixel-identical except the wavy diagnostic underline (now line
  segments) and the gutter diagnostic dot (a rounded rect); span
  backgrounds cover the font's line box, and `Style::font_scale` spans
  paint at the editor's own size.
