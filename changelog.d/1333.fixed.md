- A macOS app (native AppKit or GTK) launched from a process detached from
  the current GUI login session — most commonly a `tmux` server that
  outlives a logout/login — now prints one actionable stderr hint when
  AppKit never actually activates the window (#1333): no Dock icon, keys
  landing in the launching terminal instead of the app, and until now
  nothing printed anywhere to say why. `macos::run`'s `QuadraAppDelegate`
  and `gtk::run`'s post-launch check both sample `NSApp.isActive()`/
  `keyWindow()` once, ~1.5s after launch, through the new pure
  `macos_session_hint::activation_hint` decision function, and name `tmux`
  specifically when `$TMUX` is set. The activation sequence itself is
  unchanged; this only diagnoses the failure. Opt out with
  `QUADRAUI_NO_SESSION_HINT=1` (set by this crate's own headless lanes).
