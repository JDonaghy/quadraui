- `ListView`'s `Primitive::paint`/`paint_cells` pilot now sources its
  hover/pressed/scroll/stripe paint inputs from a `ListPaintState` read
  through the new `crate::primitive::PaintState` trait (#2852) instead of
  bare `None`/`false` literals, and `supports_border` now reflects
  `ListView::bordered` instead of always being `false` — a bordered
  `ListView` painted through the `Primitive` seam now actually strokes its
  border, with `Primitive::layout`'s row width adjusted to match.
  `Primitive::layout`'s TUI/pixel-backend discriminator now compares a
  `crate::primitive::Units` value instead of a bare `line_height <= 1.0`
  check.
