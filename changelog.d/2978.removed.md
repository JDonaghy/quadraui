- The imperative tree-table answers (#2978, 0.12 rec 14): lazily loaded
  children and a host sort order are `"tree_table_controller"` descriptor
  content now, so `quadraui-binding-core` drops
  `InteractionEngine::{sync_tree_tables, set_tree_table_children,
  set_tree_table_sort_override}`, `EventSink::dispatch_tree_table_controller`,
  `TreeTableControllerWireEvent` (now `quadraui::TreeTableModelEvent`,
  delivered through `EventSink::dispatch`), `TreeTableControllerDescriptor`,
  `TreeTableControllerNode`, `tree_table_controller_nodes_from_wire` and
  `DeferredAction::{SetTreeTableChildren, SetTreeTableSortOverride}`;
  `quadraui-py` drops `QuiApp`/`QuiAppActions.set_tree_table_children`/
  `set_tree_table_sort_override` and `quadraui-node` drops
  `QuiApp.setTreeTableChildren`/`setTreeTableSortOverride`. See each
  binding's `CHANGELOG.md` for the migration.
- From `quadraui-binding-core` (#2978): the `widget_state` module and
  its `WidgetState` store (replaced by `quadraui::WidgetStateStore`),
  `ScriptedExtra`, `ExtraPaint::as_scripted`, `scripted_region_click`,
  `scripted_key_press`, `HitDetail::CanvasRegion`, and the unused
  `button`/`point` parameters of `semantic_event_for_hit`.
- `DualModePalette::sync_caret` (#2978): the palette already paints its
  caret from its own `DualModePaletteState`, so a host that keeps that
  state itself lends it to `quadraui::with_widget_states` for the paint
  instead of writing the caret back into the descriptor.
