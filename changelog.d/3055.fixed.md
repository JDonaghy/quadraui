- `Dialog` sizes its box to its widest body line (#3055), measured with the
  real text metrics on pixel surfaces and clamped to the viewport minus the
  dialog margin, instead of a fixed 30–60 char-width box, so long confirm
  messages are no longer clipped. The cell-grid body is no longer capped at
  10 rows: it gets every row the viewport leaves after the title, table,
  input and buttons.
