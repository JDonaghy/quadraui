- `CellGrid::navigate_to(cell, &layout)` (#3082) moves the cursor to a cell
  and scrolls it into view, reporting `CellGridEvent::Navigated`. `CellGrid`
  handles the mouse wheel itself (a row per notch, a column per horizontal
  notch, cursor unmoved), and while a cell is being edited a click on it
  places the caret and a drag selects text (`CellGridState::edit_drag`,
  `CellGridPrimitiveLayout::char_width`). `Palette::show_mode_badge`
  (default `true`) hides the `[L]`/`[I]` badge.
  `FindReplacePanel::hidden_toggles` hides toggles the host cannot honour:
  they paint nothing, take no click and ignore their Alt shortcut, and
  `compute_find_replace_hit_regions_hiding` gives their slots to the input.
  `FindReplaceClickTarget::toggle` and `Color::luminance` are new helpers.
