- `MultiSectionView` implements the widget contract (#2971): `Primitive`
  lays out the chrome in the surface's own units and every expanded body
  through that body widget's own `Primitive::layout`
  (`MultiSectionViewPrimitiveLayout`, `SectionBodyView`,
  `SectionBodyLayout`), and paints each body through the body widget's
  own `paint` / `paint_cells`. `Interactive` is controlled: header clicks
  and Enter/Space report `MultiSectionViewEvent::HeaderActivated` plus
  `CollapseChanged`, divider drags `SectionResized`, scrollbar and wheel
  gestures `ScrollChanged`, aux inputs `AuxInputChanged` /
  `AuxInputSubmitted` (edited through the shared flat text-edit core),
  and every pointer, wheel and key event on a body is forwarded to the
  body widget's own `handle`, coming back as
  `MultiSectionViewEvent::Body(section, event)`. New
  `UiEvent::MultiSectionView(WidgetId, MultiSectionViewEvent)`,
  `MultiSectionViewState`, `MultiSectionViewSubPart` and
  `SectionBodyState`. `msv_metrics`, `msv_layout`, `msv_body_measure`,
  `cell_msv_metrics` and `cell_msv_layout` live in
  `primitives::multi_section_view`.
