- `TabGroup` implements the widget contract (#2955): the descriptor holds
  the panes, their tabs and the split tree (`TabGroup`, `TabGroupPane`,
  `TabGroupTab`, each generic over per-tab host content), lays the
  dividers out and paints them through a `SplitTree` child and each
  pane's strip through that pane's own `TabBar`
  (`TabGroupPrimitiveLayout`, `TabGroupPaneLayout`). `Interactive` is
  controlled: tab clicks report `TabGroupEvent::TabActivated` /
  `TabClosed` / `NewTabRequested`, a body press `PaneFocused`, divider
  drags `DividerResized { divider_idx, ratio }`, and a tab
  press-drag-release `TabDropped { tab_id, instruction }`;
  `TabGroup::apply` writes each back and reports the resulting changes.
  A tab drag shows its `DropOverlay` as a popup. Focus is one stop per
  pane (`TabGroupSubPart::Pane`). New `UiEvent::TabGroup(WidgetId,
  TabGroupEvent)`, `TabGroupState` and `TabGroupSubPart`.
- `SplitTree::split_leaf` and `SplitTree::remove_leaf` open a pane beside
  a leaf and close one, giving its space to its sibling.
