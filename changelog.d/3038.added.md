- `StatusBar`'s `Primitive::measure` reports a natural width (#3038): the
  narrowest bar its layout paints every segment in untruncated, from the
  new `StatusBar::natural_width`, so a strip sized to its content (a
  tooltip-like hint) keeps its text. `PaintSurface` gains
  `surface_push_rounded_clip` (default: the square clip) and
  `surface_overlay_scrollbar_opacity` (default `1.0`; GTK, macOS and Win
  report their idle fade). `ListViewSubPart` names a list row or its
  scrollbar as a `SubTarget`, and `ListViewPrimitiveLayout` gains
  `border_inset` and `local_hit_test`.
