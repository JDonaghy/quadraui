- **Breaking:** `Palette` and `Form` reach a frame only through the widget
  contract (#2877). `Backend::draw_palette`, `draw_palette_with_state`,
  `palette_layout`, `draw_form` and `form_layout`, the
  `tui`/`gtk`/`macos`/`win` `palette` and `form` modules (with
  `tui::draw_form`, `tui::tui_form_layout`, `tui::draw_palette`,
  `tui::tui_palette_layout`, `gtk::gtk_palette_layout`,
  `macos::mac_form_layout`, `macos::mac_palette_layout` and every
  per-backend `draw_settings_chrome` free function), and the
  `Surface::Palette`/`Surface::Form` and `FrameZone::Palette`/`FrameZone::Form`
  variants are removed. Push the widget as `Surface::Widget`, paint it with
  `DrawsPrimitives::draw_dyn` (or `draw_primitive` when the host routes its
  own pointer events), and resolve hit geometry with
  `DrawsPrimitives::layout_primitive(rect, &widget).layout`. A captured
  palette or form reports `FrameZone::Widget`. `"palette"` and `"form"` join
  `widget_kinds!`; bindings decode `"palette"` as a generic widget kind, so a
  click on a row now dispatches `Palette(id, SelectionChanged)` rather than a
  raw `MouseDown`.
- The settings-panel chrome painter is one shared painter in
  `compose::sidebar_panel_body` (`paint_settings_chrome` on a pixel surface,
  `paint_settings_chrome_cells` on a cell grid) that every
  `Backend::draw_settings_chrome` forwards to; header and query text sit
  vertically centred in their rows on every pixel backend.
- A `Form`'s `Toolbar` field paints its embedded toolbar through the
  toolbar's widget contract on every pixel backend, and pixel `Form` and
  `Palette` rows pitch off `Backend::line_height` as before.
