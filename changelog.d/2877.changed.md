- `StatusBar` and `ActivityBar` paint only through the widget contract
  (#2877). Breaking: `Backend::draw_status_bar`,
  `draw_status_bar_interactive`, `draw_status_bar_interactive_scaled`,
  `status_bar_layout`, `draw_activity_bar`, `draw_activity_bar_with_style`
  and `activity_bar_layout` are removed, along with `Surface::StatusBar`,
  `Surface::ActivityBar`, `FrameZone::StatusBar`, `FrameZone::ActivityBar`,
  the free `backend::activity_bar_hits` helper and the per-backend
  `status_bar`/`activity_bar` rasteriser modules (`tui::draw_status_bar`,
  `tui::draw_activity_bar[_with_style]`, `gtk::draw_status_bar`,
  `gtk::MIN_GAP_PX`, `gtk::ACTIVITY_ROW_PX`, `gtk::draw_activity_bar[_with_style]`,
  `macos::mac_status_bar_layout`, `macos::mac_activity_bar_layout`,
  `win::win_status_bar_layout`, `win::MIN_GAP_DIP`, `win::draw_activity_bar`,
  `win::win_activity_bar_layout`, `win::ACTIVITY_ROW_DIP`). Paint with
  `DrawsPrimitives::draw_dyn` (or push `Surface::Widget`) and read geometry
  with `DrawsPrimitives::layout_primitive`; `ActivityBarPrimitiveLayout::row_hits`
  returns the bar-relative `ActivityBarRowHit`s the removed methods returned,
  and `primitives::activity_bar::PIXEL_ROW_HEIGHT` is the pixel row height.
  The per-call font scale has no replacement. A captured bar reports
  `FrameZone::Widget`. A status bar is no longer a `ScreenLayout` tab stop
  (its widget contract takes no keyboard focus).
- `ActivityBar` gains two descriptor fields (#2877): `style: ActivityBarStyle`
  (the active-row fill and icon size the removed `_with_style` method took)
  and `hovered: Option<usize>` (the hovered `visible_items` row). Both default
  under serde; struct literals add `style: Default::default(), hovered: None`.
  `ActivityBar` no longer derives `Eq`. The activity bar's icon glyphs paint at
  `ActivityBarStyle::resolved_icon_size_px` on every pixel backend through the
  new `PaintSurface::surface_with_icon_font`, and the rail paints at its own
  rect's origin.
- A rail's keyboard focus (`ActivityBar::is_keyboard_focused`) is read from
  the frame's painted widgets at `end_frame` (#2877), so key presses still
  redirect to `UiEvent::ActivityBar(_, KeyPressed { .. })` on every backend.
  On Win-GUI a status bar painted in the custom title band needs no zone of its
  own: its painted segments read as client area in `nc_hit_test`.
- `quadraui-binding-core` decodes `"status_bar"` and `"activity_bar"` through
  `WIDGET_KINDS` (#2877); the py/node kind names and wire shapes are unchanged,
  except that `ActivityBarDescriptor` gains optional `style` and `hovered`.
  A status-bar segment now emits `StatusBar(SegmentClicked)` on release over
  the segment it was pressed on, and an activity-bar click may be preceded by
  a `FocusChanged` onto the rail.
