- `Toolbar`, `MenuBar` and `ContextMenu` lose their legacy `Backend` path
  (#2877). Breaking: `Backend::draw_toolbar_interactive`,
  `draw_toolbar_with_options`, `draw_toolbar_with_overflow`,
  `toolbar_layout`, `toolbar_layout_with_overflow`, `draw_menu_bar`,
  `draw_menu_bar_with_state`, `menu_bar_layout`, `draw_context_menu`,
  `draw_context_menu_with_state`, `draw_context_menu_with_glyphs`,
  `context_menu_border_inset` and `context_menu_viewport` are removed, as
  are `Surface::Toolbar`/`MenuBar`/`ContextMenu`, `FrameZone::Toolbar`/
  `MenuBar`/`ContextMenu` (a captured bar or popup now reports
  `FrameZone::Widget`) and the per-backend rasteriser modules with their
  free functions (`tui::draw_toolbar*`, `tui::tui_toolbar_layout*`,
  `tui::draw_menu_bar`, `tui::tui_menu_bar_layout`, `tui::draw_context_menu*`,
  `tui::CONTEXT_MENU_BORDER_INSET`, `gtk::draw_toolbar*`,
  `gtk::gtk_toolbar_layout*`, `gtk::draw_menu_bar*`, `gtk::gtk_menu_bar_layout`,
  `gtk::draw_context_menu`, `macos::toolbar`, `macos::menu_bar`,
  `macos::context_menu`, `win::draw_toolbar*`, `win::win_toolbar_layout`,
  `win::draw_menu_bar`, `win::win_menu_bar_layout`, `win::draw_context_menu`).
  Paint the widgets with `DrawsPrimitives::draw_dyn`/`draw_primitive` and
  hit-test with `layout_primitive` — a toolbar's contract layout is
  bar-local, so subtract the bar's origin before `hit_test`; read a popup's
  border reservation off `ContextMenuPrimitiveLayout::border_inset`/
  `viewport`. The native-menu services (`install_menu_bar`, `set_menu`,
  `show_context_menu`, `set_dock_menu`, the `menu_style` family) stay.
  Hover/press tinting of toolbar buttons, menu-bar items and popup rows is
  no longer painted, since the widget contract's paint carries no
  interaction state.
- `toolbar`, `menu_bar` and `context_menu` are `widget_kinds!` kinds (#2877).
- `MenuSystem::render` paints the strip through the widget contract and,
  under the native menu style, installs the bar through
  `Backend::install_menu_bar` instead; `MacBackend::install_menu_bar` skips a
  bar identical to the last one it installed, so a per-frame install does not
  rebuild the `NSMenu` tree (#2877).
