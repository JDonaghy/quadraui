- `Float::transparent` (#2558): a `bool` field, `false` by default, that
  skips the background fill `Backend::draw_float` would otherwise paint —
  a border-only (when `border` is also set) overlay through which
  whatever is already painted underneath shows. Honored by every backend
  (`tui::draw_float` and the shared `primitives::float::native_surface_paint::paint`
  GTK/macOS/Win rasteriser), and demonstrated by the `tui_float` /
  `gtk_float` example's overlay banner, which `t` cycles between
  transparent and opaque over the command list.
