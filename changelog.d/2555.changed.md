- Row presses report their mouse button (#2555). `SelectionCore::press`
  resolves a press on a `DataTable`, `ListView`, `TreeView` or `TreeTable`
  row once for every widget and binding (`RowPress`): left selects, right
  asks for the context menu, and any other button (middle, `X1`/`X2`)
  reports `DataTableEvent::RowClicked`, `ListViewEvent::ItemClicked` or
  `TreeTableEvent::RowClicked` with `button` and `modifiers`.
  `TreeEvent::RowClicked` gains `button`. `ListView` gains
  `ListViewEvent::ContextMenuRequested`, and `ListViewEvent` no longer
  derives `Eq` (it carries a `Point`).
