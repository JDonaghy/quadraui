- `Minimap` implements `Primitive` and `Interactive` (#2963), so it reaches
  a frame as `Surface::Widget` and paints through `draw_dyn` on every
  backend: braille through `paint_cells` on a cell grid, and one shared
  pixel painter on GTK, macOS and Win. A left press on the strip, and every
  move of the captured drag that follows, becomes the new
  `UiEvent::Minimap(id, MinimapEvent::Seek { fraction })`. New public items:
  `MinimapEvent`, `MinimapState`, `Minimap::cell_layout`,
  `Minimap::pixel_layout`, `primitives::minimap::resolve_cols_per_cell`,
  `BRAILLE_LINES_PER_ROW` and `BRAILLE_COLS_PER_CELL`.
- `PaintSurface` gains `surface_blit_alpha` (paint a coverage bitmap tinted
  one colour; defaults to one alpha fill per run) and
  `surface_glyph_alpha_sheet` (shape characters offscreen into a coverage
  sheet; defaults to `None`), plus the `AlphaSheet` type (#2963). GTK
  implements both with Pango and Cairo, macOS with Core Text and Core
  Graphics.
