- `FilePicker`, `FolderPicker` and `DualModePalette` are widget-contract
  widgets (#2951) composing `Palette`'s own `Primitive` and `Interactive`,
  so query editing (caret, selection, word moves) and list navigation
  come from the palette's editing core. `PathPickerState` (the former
  private `PathPickerCore`) is the pickers' public browsing model. Their
  actions reach a runtime host as the new `UiEvent::PathPicker` and
  `UiEvent::DualModePalette`.
