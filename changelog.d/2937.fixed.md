- `ListView` as a widget-contract (`Surface::Widget`) primitive: fixed a
  first Ctrl/Cmd-click discarding the descriptor's own `selected_idx`
  instead of growing its selection, `Enter`/`A11yAction::Click` activating
  the app's stale echoed `selected_idx` instead of the keyboard cursor
  after consecutive extending moves, controlled mode (an app-set
  `selected_idx`) losing to a stale internal cursor, `j`/`k` navigating
  even with Ctrl/Alt/Cmd held, and `Space` not activating the current row.
  Double-click on any `Surface::Widget` now reaches `Interactive::handle`
  as `PointerKind::DoubleClick` — `route_widget_event` had no arm for
  `UiEvent::DoubleClick` at all, so it was dead on every widget-contract
  primitive, not just `ListView`.
