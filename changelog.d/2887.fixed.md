- Widget-contract routing no longer discards the `Reaction` an
  `EventHost::emit`ted event produced when the widget answers
  `Handled::Ignored`. The event still bubbles to the remaining
  `preprocess_event` steps and `app.handle` untouched, but the merged
  reaction from those emitted events now rides back with it and is folded
  into the final `Reaction`, so an `Exit` or a short `RedrawAfter`
  deadline one of them asked for is no longer silently swallowed — by the
  declining arm, or by a later interception step (Tab cycling, Ctrl-C,
  paste, select-all) answering the bubbled event itself.
