- `DiffView` and `MessageList` implement `Primitive` and `Interactive`
  (#2970); `diff_view` registers as a widget kind in the bindings, while
  `message_list` keeps its text-region selection. A diff reports
  `UiEvent::DiffView(id, DiffViewEvent)` — `Scrolled` for the wheel and
  PageUp/PageDown, `PaneFocused` for Tab and pane clicks — and a message
  list reports `UiEvent::MessageList(id, MessageListEvent::Scrolled {
  scroll_top, at_bottom })` for the wheel, `at_bottom` driving follow-tail.
  `scroll_offset`, `focused_pane` and `scroll_top` stay descriptor fields.
  New public items: `DiffViewEvent`, `DiffViewPrimitiveLayout`,
  `MessageListEvent`, and `MessageListLayout::bounds` / `line_height`.
