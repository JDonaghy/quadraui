- `Chart` implements `Primitive` and `Interactive` (#2967), so it reaches a
  frame as `Surface::Widget` and paints through `draw_dyn` on every backend.
  Hover and crosshair are app-owned descriptor fields: `Chart` gains
  `hovered_point` and `crosshair_x` (both `#[serde(default)]`), and the
  chart reports the point under the pointer as the new
  `ChartEvent::DataPointHovered { id, point }`. A press reports
  `DataPointClicked`/`LegendClicked`/`Clicked`; the arrow keys step the
  hover through the points and Enter clicks it. New public items:
  `ChartPrimitiveLayout`, `ChartState`, `Chart::layout_in` and
  `Chart::series_color`.
- `Canvas` implements `Primitive` and `Interactive` (#2967): a declared
  region's hover, press and double-click become `UiEvent::Canvas` events,
  and the pointer takes the region's `CanvasCursor`. On a cell surface with
  a graphics channel, an image op paints real pixels. New public item:
  `CanvasState`.
- The `"chart"` binding kind decodes as a generic widget leaf, so its
  clicks and hover reach bindings as typed `Chart` events (#2967).
