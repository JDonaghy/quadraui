- `MouseButton` is now `#[non_exhaustive]` (#2779): a downstream `match` on
  `quadraui::MouseButton` must add a wildcard arm (`_ => {}`) to keep
  compiling. Construction (`MouseButton::Left`, `MouseButton::Other(3)`) and
  single-variant destructuring (`if let MouseButton::Left = button`) are
  unaffected. Grepping `vimcode` and `coord-tui` found no exhaustive
  `MouseButton` match lacking a wildcard arm already, so neither needs a
  migration.
