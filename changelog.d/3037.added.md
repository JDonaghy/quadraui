- Cell-grid look options for the widget contract (#3037):
  `TabChrome::separator` / `TabChrome::with_separator` with the new
  `TabSeparator::{Rule, None}` (`None` drops the `│` column after a tab with
  no close glyph); `ControlWidth::ContentEnd`, a content-sized form control
  placed against the row's right edge (two blank cells before the edge on
  TUI) for `Toggle`, `TextInput`, `PasswordInput`, `TextArea`, `ReadOnly`,
  `ColorPicker`, `Dropdown`, `ComboBox` and `DatePicker`, plus
  `ControlWidth::place`; and `TreeStyle::show_scrollbar` (default `true`),
  which when off gives a TUI tree's last column to its rows and paints no
  scrollbar on pixel backends.
