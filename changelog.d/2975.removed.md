- The group-F legacy paint path is removed (#2975): the `Backend` methods
  `draw_multi_section_view`, `msv_layout`, `msv_metrics`, `draw_chart`,
  `chart_layout`, `draw_canvas`, `canvas_layout`, `draw_split`,
  `split_layout`, `draw_split_tree`, `split_tree_layout`, `draw_board`,
  `board_layout`, `draw_pipeline_view`, `pipeline_view_layout`,
  `draw_diff_view`, `diff_view_layout`, `draw_message_list`,
  `draw_data_table`, `draw_data_table_with_state`,
  `draw_data_table_with_glyphs` and `data_table_layout`; the per-backend
  `tui`/`gtk`/`macos`/`win` wrapper files for those widgets (with their
  `tui::draw_*`/`tui_*_layout`, `gtk_*_layout`, `mac_*_layout`,
  `win_*_layout`, `gtk::scroll_view` and `win::scroll_view` re-exports);
  and the `Surface`/`FrameZone` variants `MultiSectionView`, `Split`,
  `SplitTree`, `DataTable`, `Chart`, `Canvas`, `Board`, `DiffView`,
  `MessageList` and `PipelineView` (a captured `OwnedContent` of each now
  maps to `FrameZone::Widget`). Paint these widgets with
  `DrawsPrimitives::draw_dyn`/`draw_primitive`, read their geometry with
  `layout_primitive`, and push them as `Surface::Widget`. A chart's hover
  and crosshair are the descriptor's `hovered_point`/`crosshair_x`; a data
  table's hovered row, stripe and pixel scroll are a `DataTablePaintState`
  passed to `draw_dyn`, and its sort glyphs are `DataTable::glyphs`.
  `ScrollView`'s painters are `primitives::scroll_view::{paint_clipped,
  draw_scrollbar_for}`.
- `SidebarSystem::set_backend_info` drops its unused `MsvLayoutMetrics`
  argument.
- `Backend::draw_find_replace`, the `tui::draw_find_replace` rasteriser
  and the `Surface::FindReplace`/`FrameZone::FindReplace` variants are
  removed (#2975); a captured `OwnedContent::FindReplace` maps to
  `FrameZone::Widget`. Paint a `FindReplacePanel` with
  `DrawsPrimitives::draw_dyn` in the box
  `FindReplacePanel::anchored_rect` places, and push it as
  `Surface::Widget`.
