- `Button::hovered`/`Button::pressed` (#3008): host-set `bool` fields, same
  convention `Switch::hovered`/`Switch::pressed` already follow, read by
  `Button`'s shared pixel/cell painters (reached through
  `Primitive::paint`/`Primitive::paint_cells`) to tint the background
  toward `Theme::button_hover_bg`/`Theme::button_pressed_bg`. A host feeds
  them from its own `InteractionState` the same way
  `examples/common/controls.rs` does.
