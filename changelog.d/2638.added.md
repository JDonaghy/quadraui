- GTK rasteriser for `ToolbarOverflow::Wrap` and `ToolbarOverflow::Menu`
  (#2638): `GtkBackend::draw_toolbar_with_overflow` /
  `toolbar_layout_with_overflow` now override the default Clip-only
  forward with new `gtk::draw_toolbar_with_overflow` /
  `gtk_toolbar_layout_with_overflow` functions. `Wrap` stacks overflowing
  top-level items onto additional rows `line_height` pixels apart — any
  row the host's `h` doesn't cover is still reported for
  `ToolbarLayout::cross_extent` probing but marked un-clickable, matching
  what the Cairo clip at `(x, y, w, h)` actually paints. `Menu` paints the
  trailing `»` affordance through the shared
  `native_surface_paint::paint`'s `ToolbarItemKind::Overflow` arm (now
  handled there instead of silently skipped), which GTK, macOS, and
  Win-GUI all share — a click on it hit-tests to `ToolbarHit::Overflow`,
  ready to open a `ContextMenu` from `ToolbarLayout::overflowed_items`.
