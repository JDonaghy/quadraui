- A content-sized stack measures its children: `NaturalSize::stacked`
  adds the children's main-axis sizes and gaps and takes their largest
  cross-axis size. The Rust app layer's `column`/`row` and the Python and
  Node `Column`/`Row` (`size="content"`) share it, so a content-sized row
  of buttons takes its buttons' height in every language instead of
  collapsing in the bindings (#3107).
