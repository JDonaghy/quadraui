- `DataTable` is a full widget-contract widget (#2966): `impl Interactive`
  with `DataTableState` (multi-selection, typeahead, column cursor, divider
  and scrollbar-thumb drags). Arrow/Page/Home/End navigation scrolls the
  cursor row into view, Left/Right move a column cursor, typing jumps to a
  row by its first column, clicks honour Ctrl/Cmd and Shift, a header
  divider drag resizes, and the wheel and scrollbar track scroll. New
  `DataTableEvent::{ScrollChanged, ColumnFocused, ContextMenuRequested}`,
  `DataTableSubPart`, and `DataTableHit::Scrollbar` /
  `TreeTableHit::Scrollbar`.
- `DataTable::striped` and `DataTable::glyphs` descriptor fields; `striped`
  is on the py/node wire descriptor.
- `DataTableLayout::v_scrollbar_track` (the one track every painter draws
  and `hit_test` resolves) and `DataTableLayout::scroll_sub_px`.
- `ColumnWidth::Native(f32)`: N cells on TUI, N pixels on GTK/macOS/Win
  (#3039).
- `ListViewPrimitiveLayout::{vscrollbar, vscrollbar_layout}` and the single
  list layout resolver `ListView::resolve_layout` (#3039).
- `SelectionCore` (shared multi-selection anchor + set),
  `Typeahead::push_now` / `Typeahead::with_clock` and
  `behaviour::typeahead::monotonic_now_ms`.
