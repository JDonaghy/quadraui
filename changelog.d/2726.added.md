- `CellGrid::cells_window` and `CellGrid::cells_requested` — two-axis
  virtualisation for the spreadsheet-style `CellGrid` primitive: the former
  reports the absolute sheet block the scrolled (non-frozen) portion of
  `CellGrid::cells` currently covers, based on the new
  `CellGrid::loaded_row_start`/`CellGrid::loaded_col_start` fields (set by
  whichever caller filled `cells`, independent of `row_scroll`/
  `col_scroll` so a scroll or a jump past the loaded block is always
  detectable); the latter compares that window against a resolved
  `CellGridLayout` and returns a new `CellGridEvent::CellsRequested {
  row_start, col_start, rows, cols }` when the loaded block no longer
  covers the viewport, so a host can supply only the visible slab of a
  sheet with millions of rows and tens of thousands of columns (#2726).
