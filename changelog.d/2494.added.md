- `quadraui::BAR_HEIGHT`/`resolve_bar_height` (#2494): the in-canvas
  `MenuBar` strip's fixed pixel-backend height (30px, VS Code's custom
  title bar) as a metric token, instead of deriving it from the backend's
  own chrome line height. `resolve_bar_height(candidate_line_height)`
  floors `candidate_line_height` at `BAR_HEIGHT` whenever
  `candidate_line_height > 1.0` (GTK/macOS/Win — so a chrome font taller
  than 30px still gets the room it measured) and passes the candidate
  through unchanged on TUI's `1.0`-per-row cell grid. A caller builds its
  `bounds.height` for `MenuBar::layout`/`layout_with_leading` from this
  helper instead of a raw line-height measurement; existing callers are
  unaffected until they opt in.
  `quadraui-gallery`'s `MenuBarDemo` and the `menu_bar_app`/`submenu_app`
  example apps now derive their bar rect the same way, so their
  macOS/GTK/Win capture shows the taller bar.
