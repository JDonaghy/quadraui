- `quadraui::BAR_HEIGHT`/`resolve_bar_height` (#2494): the in-canvas
  `MenuBar` strip's fixed pixel-backend height (30px, VS Code's custom
  title bar) as a metric token, instead of deriving it from the backend's
  own chrome line height. `resolve_bar_height(candidate_line_height)`
  returns `BAR_HEIGHT` whenever `candidate_line_height > 1.0` (GTK/macOS/
  Win) and passes the candidate through unchanged on TUI's `1.0`-per-row
  cell grid. A caller builds its `bounds.height` for
  `MenuBar::layout`/`layout_with_leading` from this helper instead of a
  raw line-height measurement; existing callers are unaffected until they
  opt in.
