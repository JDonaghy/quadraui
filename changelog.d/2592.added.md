- `IconGrid`'s GUI tile painter (#2592) now paints `IconGridItem::image`
  (scaled to fit the icon band, aspect preserved, falling back to the
  codicon glyph when the backend can't yet decode the thumbnail), wraps a
  tile's label to at most 2 lines before ellipsizing, and fills the
  selected/hovered tile with a rounded highlight plus a focus ring —
  `row_stack::row_content_paint::paint_tile_icon_label`'s new
  `TileHighlight` parameter, shared by every GUI backend through the same
  `native_surface_paint` path GTK/macOS/Win already paint `IconGrid`
  through.
