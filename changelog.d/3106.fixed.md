- The Rust app layer (`quadraui::app`) matches the Python and Node
  engines where it did not (#3106): Tab repaints the focus ring at once
  instead of on the next event; Tab or Shift+Tab at a form's last or first
  field moves focus on to the next widget instead of wrapping inside the
  form (`FocusSpec::child_at_edge`, shared with binding-core, and
  `FocusTree::sync_tab_order` keeping a child-focused widget's place in
  the cycle); `q` and Escape no longer quit while a focused list keeps the
  key, and Ctrl-C still does; a content-sized `row`/`column` with
  `padding` or margin measures them (`Spacing::outset`), so its children
  no longer vanish; and `Run` paints and lists tab stops from its kept
  frame by reference, copying the widgets only for `frame_tree`.
