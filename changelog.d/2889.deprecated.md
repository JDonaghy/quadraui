- `frame::FrameHitMap`/`frame::FrameZone` (#2889): superseded by
  `crate::frame_tree::FrameTree` (via `ScreenLayout::into_frame_tree`/
  `AppLogic::frame_tree`) for `Surface::Widget` click/hit-test routing;
  every other `Surface` variant's hit-testing moves to the app recomputing
  its own rects, the way `examples/common/frame_demo.rs` now does.
  `ScreenLayout::hit_map`/`OwnedFrame::hit_map` are deprecated too — their
  only in-tree consumer (`examples/common/hit_map_recover_demo.rs` and its
  `tui_hit_map_recover`/`gtk_hit_map_recover` runners) is removed.
  `ScreenLayout::draw`/`OwnedFrame::draw` still compile and still return a
  `FrameHitMap` for now (both carry a scoped `#[allow(deprecated)]`); this
  is PR 1 of the two-PR protocol — removal (PR 2) waits on vimcode's own
  migration off `FrameHitMap`/`FrameZone`.
