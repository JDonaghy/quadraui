- New `CellSurface` trait (#2815): the cell-grid counterpart of `PaintSurface`
  — `size`, `put_char`, `put_wide`, `put_str_clipped`, `fill`, `draw_border`,
  `get`, `set_cursor`, `emit_graphics`, `color_depth`. `TuiBackend`'s TUI
  rasteriser is the first implementor, reached off the new
  `Backend::cell_surface`, which replaces `Backend::cell_buffer` (same method
  count on `Backend`; `DrawsPrimitives::draw_primitive` now reaches the
  `ratatui::buffer::Buffer` it needs for `Primitive::paint_cells` through
  `CellSurface::ratatui_buffer`, a `#[doc(hidden)]` migration hatch deleted
  once every TUI rasteriser has moved onto `CellSurface` itself). Additive
  only: no existing `tui/<widget>.rs` rasteriser paints through it yet.
