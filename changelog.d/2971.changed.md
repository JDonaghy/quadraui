- `MultiSectionView` section bodies paint through their own widgets on
  every backend (#2971): message-list bodies now paint on macOS, terminal
  bodies paint on every backend instead of being left to the host, and
  form bodies paint their toolbar fields through the form's own painter.
  A tree or list body whose section shows a scrollbar gutter no longer
  paints a second scrollbar of its own (GTK and Windows painted both).
  Pixel backends paint section headers on whole-pixel rows (GTK already
  did). A message with no text measures one header line plus one text
  line on a cell grid, as on a pixel surface.
- **Breaking:** `primitives::layout_metrics::{msv_metrics, msv_layout,
  msv_body_measure}` moved to `primitives::multi_section_view`.
  `HeaderHit` and `AuxHit` derive `Serialize`/`Deserialize`.
