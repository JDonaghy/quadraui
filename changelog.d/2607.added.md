- `TreeView`, `DataTable` and `TreeTable` implement `quadraui::Primitive`
  (#2607), joining `ListView`'s pilot (#2646): each routes `Primitive::paint`
  through its existing `native_surface_paint::paint`/`paint_with_state`
  helper (pixel output unchanged) and `Primitive::paint_cells` straight to
  its existing TUI `draw_*` rasteriser. `TreeTable`'s impl composes
  `DataTable`'s own layout/paint plus the shared tree cell — no parallel
  row, column or scrollbar logic of its own — and also gains its first
  `A11yPrimitive` impl, delegating to the wrapped `DataTable`'s.
