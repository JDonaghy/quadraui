- `TreeView`, `DataTable` and `TreeTable` implement `quadraui::Primitive`
  (#2607), joining `ListView`'s pilot (#2646): each routes `Primitive::paint`
  through its existing `native_surface_paint::paint`/`paint_with_state`
  helper (pixel output unchanged) and `Primitive::paint_cells` straight to
  its existing TUI `draw_*` rasteriser, verified byte-for-byte against that
  rasteriser's own output by a `TuiBackend`-routed test for each of the
  three (mirroring the pilot's TUI parity tests). `TreeTable`'s impl
  composes `DataTable`'s own layout/paint plus the shared tree cell via
  the new `layout_metrics::pixel_tree_table_layout` — no parallel row,
  column or scrollbar logic of its own — and also gains its first
  `A11yPrimitive` impl, delegating to the wrapped `DataTable`'s. On a GUI
  backend, `DataTable`'s and `TreeTable`'s `Primitive::layout` resolves
  column/chevron widths from `LayoutCtx::char_width` (an estimate); the
  pixel paint that actually lands re-measures from the live surface, so
  the returned layout's hit geometry is approximate until `LayoutCtx`
  carries a live text measurer.
