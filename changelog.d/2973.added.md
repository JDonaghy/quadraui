- `Checkbox`, `Slider` and `Select`: standalone core widgets on the widget
  contract (`Primitive` + `Interactive`), the standalone forms of a form
  row's `Toggle`, `Slider` and `Dropdown` kinds. Each paints through the
  same per-kind control painter a `Form` row uses (extracted from
  `form/cell_paint.rs` and `form.rs`'s pixel painter as
  `paint_bare_field`), shares that row's key handling
  (`checkbox::activates`, `slider::step_for_key`, `select::step_for_key`)
  and reports the same `FormEvent` (`ToggleChanged`, `SliderChanged`,
  `DropdownChanged`). A `Slider` press sets the value under the pointer on
  its painted track and a drag follows it. `BareFieldLayout` is their
  shared layout.
