- `"row"`/`"column"` layout-tree nodes gain a `wrap: bool` key (#2525), additive
  via `opt_bool` defaulting to `false` on the wire (an existing render callback
  with no `"wrap"` key keeps laying every child out on a single line). With
  `wrap: true`, children flow onto a new line, stacked along the container's
  cross axis, once the next child would overflow the main-axis extent; each
  line resolves its own `justify`/`align` independently. Rust's
  `crate::layout::LayoutContainer` gains a matching `wrap: bool` field — not
  additive at the Rust struct-literal level (every construction site must now
  list it), but `LayoutContainer` is `quadraui-binding-core`-internal with no
  in-tree or published consumer outside this crate.
