- `Backend::draw_radio_group`/`Backend::radio_group_layout` and the
  per-backend `tui`/`macos`/`win` `radio_group` rasteriser modules are
  removed (#3007): `RadioGroup` already paints and lays out through the
  widget contract (`Primitive` + `Interactive`, #3005), so every caller —
  `tui/form.rs`'s embedded-field body, `examples/common/controls.rs`, the
  three `*_controls.rs` example runners and the four
  `*_standalone_controls_driver.rs`/`conformance/c0.rs` test suites — now
  calls `DrawsPrimitives::draw_primitive`/`layout_primitive` instead of a
  `Backend` method. `quadraui-binding-core` drops `RadioGroup`'s
  `ExtraPaint` impl and its hand-written `radio_group_key_event` dispatch
  arm in favor of the same generic `WIDGET_KINDS`/`PaintedHit::Widget` seam
  `IconGrid`/`CellGrid`/`Switch` already use; an arrow-key move now emits
  `UiEvent::RadioGroupSelectionChanged` instead of
  `UiEvent::Form(.., FormEvent::SegmentedControlChanged)`, and a standalone
  `RadioGroup` click now resolves straight to the clicked option (it
  previously only focused the whole group, with no per-option hit test
  available at that layer). Neither removed `Backend` method had a caller
  in any published consumer.
