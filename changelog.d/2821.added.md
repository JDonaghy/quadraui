- Added `quadraui::behaviour`: the single shared implementation of
  scroll-into-view (`behaviour::scroll::ensure_visible`/`ensure_visible_px`),
  list/tree navigation (`behaviour::nav::FlatNav`, `behaviour::nav::NavKey`,
  and `behaviour::nav::TreeNavCore`/`NavRow`, moved from the now-removed
  `compose::tree_nav`), and typeahead (`behaviour::typeahead::Typeahead`)
  (#2821). `FormController::scroll_to_field`, `PathPickerCore::sync_scroll`,
  `DualModePaletteController::sync_scroll`, `Palette::resolved_scroll_offset`,
  `IconGrid::layout`, `CellGridController::ensure_cursor_visible`,
  `TextInput::layout`'s vertical and horizontal scroll clamp, and
  `ComboBox::move_highlight` now call into `ensure_visible`/`FlatNav` instead
  of each re-deriving the same clamp/wrap arithmetic. `PathPickerCore`'s
  `move_up`/`move_down`, `MenuSystem`'s `next_enabled_menu`/`move_selection`,
  `ContextMenuController::move_selection`, `ActivityBarKeyboard`'s
  `select_next`/`select_prev`, and `Workspace::cycle` also now go through
  `FlatNav` rather than their own clamped/wrapping index-move copy, and
  `ComboBox` gains `typeahead_highlight` for a buffered, debounced
  type-to-jump alternative to its live text filter.
