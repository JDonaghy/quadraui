- `Editor` implements `Primitive` and `Interactive` (#2959), so it reaches a
  frame as `Surface::Widget` and paints through `draw_dyn` on every backend.
  Its layout uses the `rect` it is given and the editor font's metrics; its
  cell paint lives in the widget and reports a `Bar`/`Underline` caret
  through `CellSurface::set_cursor`. A press, drag, double-click or wheel
  gesture becomes the new `UiEvent::Editor(id, EditorEvent)`, carrying the
  `EditorHit` and monospace column under the pointer; keys and text bubble to
  the app. New public items: `EditorEvent`, `EditorState`,
  `Editor::paint_options` / `Editor::with_paint_options`.
