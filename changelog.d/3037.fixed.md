- A TUI `TabBar` painted through the widget contract keeps its active tab
  on screen (#3037): when `scroll_offset` would hide it, the cell layout
  starts the strip at `TabBar::fit_active_scroll_offset`'s offset and
  reports it as `corrected_scroll_offset`, so a host no longer probes
  offsets itself. `StatusBar::layout` no longer ellipsis-truncates left
  text that fits beside the right group: the minimum gap shrinks first.
