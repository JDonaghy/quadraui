- `FocusManager`, `AppLogic::tab_stops` (deprecated since 0.12.0, see #2909)
  and `compose::FocusRing`/`compose::focus_ring` are removed (#2911), now
  that `FocusTree` (#2908-#2910) is the one focus surface every backend and
  in-tree consumer drives. `Backend::focus_manager` is replaced by
  `Backend::focus_tree`, returning a `&FocusTree`; `Backend::set_focus`
  keeps its existing signature, now backed by `FocusTree` internally. Every
  in-tree override of the deleted `AppLogic::tab_stops` (the TUI/GTK focus
  and form-groups examples, the gallery's Focus/Form demos, and
  `quadraui-binding-core`/`quadraui-node`/`quadraui-py`'s own `AppLogic`
  wrappers) is migrated to `AppLogic::extra_tab_stops`.
