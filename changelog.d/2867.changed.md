- **Breaking:** `RichTextPopup` carries a `font_role: FontRole` field
  (#2867). It replaces the per-call
  `Backend::draw_rich_text_popup_with_font_role` role request on the
  widget-contract path: `Primitive::font_role` reports it, and
  `Primitive::measure`/`Primitive::layout` measure the body and the link
  hit regions through it, so an editor-role popup is sized in the editor
  font instead of always the chrome font. A consumer with an exhaustive
  `RichTextPopup` literal adds `font_role: FontRole::Chrome` (or
  `Default::default()`) to keep today's metrics; frames deserialized
  without the field also default to `FontRole::Chrome`. `FontRole` itself
  now derives `Default` (`Chrome`), `Serialize` and `Deserialize` so the
  descriptor round-trips.
