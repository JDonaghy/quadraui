- `ComboBox::layout`, `ComboBox::click`, `ComboBoxLayout` and `ComboBoxHit`
  (#3028). The dropdown is the box's own popup now, so a host no longer
  lays it out or hit-tests it; a host that routes clicks itself commits a
  row with the new `ComboBox::pick(index)`. `ComboBoxPrimitiveLayout`'s
  `layout` field is replaced by `field` and `dropdown_rows`.
