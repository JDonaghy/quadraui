- `TuiBackend::draw_image` now paints real pixels on a terminal with
  confirmed Kitty-protocol graphics support (`tui-images` Cargo feature,
  truecolor): it decodes through `tui::image_decode`, fills the placed
  cells with `tui::kitty_graphics`'s Unicode placeholder content, queues
  that image's transmit/placement bytes, and reports
  `ImagePaintResult::Painted` (#2576) — the frame-loop wiring for the
  previously-unwired decode (#2574) and protocol-byte (#2575) modules.
  `tui::run::flush_kitty_graphics_writes` flushes the queued bytes to the
  real terminal right after `terminal.draw` returns, and
  `teardown_terminal` deletes every transmitted image on exit. Falls back
  to today's `Image::fallback_text` path and
  `ImagePaintResult::Unsupported` on a bare `tui` build, an unconfirmed
  terminal, or a real decode failure. The TUI `Canvas` `DrawOp::Image` op
  is upgraded the same way.
