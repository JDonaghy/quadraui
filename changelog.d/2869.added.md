- `NumberInput`, `ComboBox` and `DatePicker` implement the widget
  contract (#2869): `Primitive` (natural-size `measure`, layout, pixel
  paint and cell paint all in the widget's own module) plus
  `Interactive`, so each can be pushed as a `Surface::Widget` and
  registered in a `FrameTree` instead of only being drawn by `Form`.
  `NumberInput` steps on a stepper click, a wheel notch and `Up`/`Down`,
  edits a typed draft and commits it clamped on `Enter`. `ComboBox`
  opens its list on `Alt+Down`, a closed-box `Down`/`Enter` or a field
  click, navigates it through `behaviour::nav::FlatNav`, jumps the
  highlight to the best prefix match through
  `behaviour::typeahead::Typeahead` as you type, and commits on `Enter`
  or a row click. `DatePicker` moves its cursor by day and week with the
  arrow keys, by month with `PageUp`/`PageDown` or the header's `<`/`>`
  controls, and confirms on `Enter` or a day click. Every outcome rides
  `UiEvent::Form` under the widget's own id, on the `FormEvent` channels
  that already name it.
- `FormEvent::DateFocusMoved` (#2869) reports a `DatePicker`'s cursor
  moving to a different day without confirming it — the scrubbing
  `DatePickerEvent::FocusMoved` carries, which had no `UiEvent` channel
  before. `FormEvent::DateChanged` stays the confirm-only channel.
- `quadraui::tui::draw_number_input`/`draw_combo_box`/`draw_date_picker`
  are now thin delegates onto the widget-owned cell painters (#2869),
  which paint through `CellSurface` instead of writing cells directly.
